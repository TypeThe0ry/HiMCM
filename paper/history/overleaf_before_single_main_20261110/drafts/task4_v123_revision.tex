\subsection{A significant revision: retaining uncertain evidence}
A controlled comparison shows why the evidence bounds belong in the decision.
On the same 160 utility entries, we compare the midpoint completion
$z_{ij}=(\ell_{ij}+h_{ij})/2$ with the retained-interval formulation in
Equation~\ref{eq:regret}. Both use identical weights, anchors and TOPSIS scores.
The midpoint is a declared diagnostic scenario, rather than an estimate of
missing observations or a reconstruction of the team's historical first model.

The revision retains 48 non-point entries and selects the city with the smallest
worst-case shortfall. Zurich remains the recommendation, but thirteen cities
change decision rank under fixed weights; Kendall's $\tau_b=0.600$.
Dublin, Helsinki and Paris rank second, third and fourth at the midpoint,
whereas Singapore, Oslo and Stockholm occupy those positions in the interval
decision. With equal weights, fifteen ranks change and $\tau_b=0.667$.

A constructed interior completion demonstrates the consequence. Keep the other
cities at their utility midpoints and set each uncertain Zurich utility to
$\ell_{ij}+0.1(h_{ij}-\ell_{ij})$. All 112 point values stay fixed, and each
of the 48 uncertain values is strictly inside its interval. Dublin then scores
62.31483 and Zurich 55.54326, putting Zurich thirteenth in that completion.
At the more adverse rectangular endpoints, Dublin's 70.90353 exceeds Zurich's
53.53548 by exactly 17.36805, realizing Zurich's model maximum regret.
Some endpoints represent raw-scale suprema; these constructions concern the
declared utility rectangle, rather than an observed population or a proof of
joint physical attainability.

This revision improves the interpretation of the decision: one chosen completion
identifies a conditional first place, while the interval rule retains all sixteen
possible champions and still selects a conservative action. It changes the
handling of uncertainty within one scoring framework. The earlier 13.97\%
interval-width reduction comes separately from admitted data, not from this
controlled model comparison.
