\begin{center}{\Large\bfseries Evidence update: scoped facility inventories}\\[6pt]
V124 companion to the independent V123 review draft
\end{center}
Two verified facility inventories improve precision while preserving the city
recommendation. Madrid's registered metro subset and five public indoor pools
in Tokyo replace two fully missing entries with lower bounds. The original
city table, all 139 previously non-fully-missing entries, weights and utility
anchors stay fixed. The resulting matrix contains 112 point values, 29 partial
intervals and 19 fully missing entries.

The average fixed-weight score-interval width falls from \WidthBefore{} to

\section*{Count facilities inside the comparison boundary}
Madrid's regional transport authority (CRTM) publishes a station registry with
municipality codes and network-station identifiers \cite{crtm}. The original
293 records include 242 tagged to Madrid city (province 28, municipality 079,
independently identified by INE \cite{ine}). Two unnamed records with no route
information are omitted. Consolidating the remaining 240 named records by
network-station code gives 191 metro complexes. Noviciado/Plaza de Espa\~na and
Embajadores/Acacias are each conservatively counted once. The fifty-one
outside-city records do not enter the count.

This is a 29 May 2026 registered inventory divided by the unchanged supplied
2025 population of 3,527,924. Its transport utility is
$[0.09770010875,1]$ under the existing anchor 50. Other modes and providers
remain uncounted, and the registry does not establish real-time operation.
It therefore supplies a conditional lower bound rather than a complete
same-year city transport census.

\section*{Distinguish current closures from planned closures}
Tokyo's retained subset consists of five municipal indoor swimming venues at
different addresses in Nerima, one of the twenty-three special wards
\cite{nerima}. Each facility's original municipal page confirms a 25-metre
pool and regular opening information. The current closure of Heiwadai, with
reopening undetermined, excludes it. Miharadai's announced February--May 2027
closure is a future change and does not remove it from the current stock
\cite{closures}. Counting venues once avoids adding lanes or separate basins.
With the unchanged supplied population of 9,983,365, the existing pool anchor
1 gives $[0.04769460951,1]$. Other wards and providers remain unknown.
Routine maintenance days and restricted sessions mean that this stock count
does not imply simultaneous opening or individual adolescent access.

\section*{Direct further searches toward decisions}
A separate acquisition diagnostic holds the pre-update V121 evidence fixed.
It distinguishes reducing the possible-champion set from changing the
conservative recommendation. An exact, correctly scoped Dublin pupil--teacher
ratio below 17.58238 would change the fixed-weight recommendation to Dublin;
this is a conditional threshold, not an observed ratio. Zurich remains the
fixed-weight recommendation for an exact ratio below 175.33693 and remains
the equal-weight choice throughout the admissible ratio range. Interval-valued
teacher evidence requires the full two-bound decision calculation.

A matched teacher denominator remains the key requirement. The inspected Zurich
series covers canton-level publicly funded schools \cite{zurichstaff}; Dublin's
official report says school-level FTE are not published \cite{irelandstaff}. These sources do not establish a citywide, matching
teacher denominator. Their missing cells remain unknown; a source's existence
alone does not make its population compatible.

\section*{Controlled verification and manuscript scope}
Independent weighted norms and explicit competitor exclusion reproduce twenty
static comparisons and twenty-four weight-simulation settings, each with
10,000 draws. The original random streams are retained, and the pre-update
arm matches saved V121 results. Withdrawing both supplements restores every
V121 utility bound. Weight-simulation frequencies remain conditional on their
chosen distributions, rather than actual winning probabilities.

This companion supplies new data-collection and baseline evidence. The V123
complete review paper and V122 preference, future and method results retain
their explicitly frozen V121 inputs. Their numerical claims have not been
silently relabelled V124; integrating the new evidence requires rerunning those
extensions and updating the paper consistently. Team control and the complete
AI-use record remain pending, so this companion is research manuscript material,
not a final submission. OpenAI Codex assisted extraction checks, calculation and
writing \cite{codex}; it does not provide missing empirical observations.
