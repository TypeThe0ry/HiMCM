# V150 Madrid teacher-count source review

A new candidate municipal series supplies 20 end-year labels, 2005–2024, corresponding to school years 2004/05–2023/24. The final label is retained as 2024 (P). Madrid's final count is 1,008, compared with 1,922 for the whole Comunidad. The published unit is Número; city upper-secondary teaching FTE and a matched pupil denominator are not established. No observation is admitted and no ratio is constructed.

The saved provider extraction contains a regional row, 11 statistical zones and 179 municipalities. Every year reconciles across the three levels, and the Madrid municipality row equals the Municipio de Madrid zone row. Adding all levels in 2023/24 would give 5,766, three times the regional total. The candidate CSV preserves the source year labels, explicitly false FTE/admission flags and separate reconciliation columns. It is not a replacement evaluation matrix or a new city sample.

The official report describes exclusive Bachillerato teaching separately. Its regional all-Régimen-General FTE ratio of 12.6 is not a city upper-stage ratio. Numerical agreement between regional counts does not by itself establish identical stage definitions. The municipal note establishes that the label is the final school-course year. No count-to-FTE conversion, year relabelling or future trend is assumed.

The archived material is a serialized Exa response containing provider-extracted official HTML/PDF text, not original HTTP bodies. Six direct requests yielded no saved original; a further CSV provider request timed out. No count is inferred from those failures. PDF charts include noisy text and unverified cell order, so only readable scope prose is used.
