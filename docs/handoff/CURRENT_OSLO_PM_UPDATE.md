# Oslo PM2.5: keep station observations and modelled exposure distinct

Two primary datasets clarify the scope needed to verify the retained **7.9 µg/m³ / 2024 / MODELLED** point. Neither establishes that original value's extraction lineage. New primary admissions and actual primary interval-width reduction are both zero; V203 and the current manuscript remain unchanged.

The [Oslo MIL032 table](https://statistikkbanken.oslo.kommune.no/statbank/pxweb/en/db1/db1__Klima%20og%20milj%C3%B8__Luftkvalitet/OK-MIL032.px/) returns 20 station cells for 2024: 12 reported annual concentrations and eight missing values. The notes distinguish inactive stations, absent data and less than 75% coverage as possible causes of blanks, without assigning a cause to each blank. The data supply station measurements, not an Oslo population-weighted exposure. No station average is calculated or substituted.

The archived [OECD Air pollution – Cities and FUAs query](https://sdmx.oecd.org/public/rest/v1/data/OECD.CFE.EDS,DSD_FUA_ENV@DF_POLLUTION,1.2/NO001C+NO001F.A.PM25_POP_EXP.MCG_M3...?dimensionAtObservation=AllDimensions) returns six annual observations for 2021–2023. Its 2023 city value is 8.506857989031039 and its FUA value 7.934470167063854 µg/m³; the latter rounds to 7.9 at the source's stated one-decimal presentation precision. It is a different year and a functional urban area, so this numerical match cannot identify the original 2024 row. No 2024 observation appears in this targeted response; that is not a claim about every OECD product or version.

OECD's archived metadata describes Plume Labs pollution grids and GHS-POP population weighting. City and FUA geography differ, and the exact Oslo boundary and population-grid vintages were not established. These estimates are separate from the municipal station series and from the EEA viewer candidate. A same-name city or similar number is insufficient to reconcile them.

Five successful response bodies and two failed endpoint responses are preserved under `research/2026-a/data/current_oslo_pm_stations/` and `research/2026-a/data/current_oslo_pm_model/`. Receipts include the exact read-only query body and source hashes. Failed endpoint responses are not measurement gaps. Both extractors print deterministic selections; `scripts/audit_current_oslo_pm_sources.py` checks receipts, dimensions, metadata, years, values and the untouched user point. An independent reviewer also checked the raw JSON-stat and CSV records. The verification protects 548 scientific bindings, 77 supplementary bindings, 10 current withdrawal-evidence entries and seven manuscript dependencies in both checkouts.

The next qualifying source must identify the modelled 2024 product, its actual Oslo record and aggregation geography. Keep the retained CSV value and flag until an explicitly justified admission or controlled alternative is reviewed; do not infer an error from a different observed or modelled measure. The selected source-route index now contains 16 entries to prevent repeated use of these near-matches.
