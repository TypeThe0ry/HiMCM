# Bounded staff and duration source follow-up

This follow-up preserves five new official response bodies and turns the Zurich teacher-data gap into a specific aggregate request. It supplies no new primary observation: the active V203 score intervals, recommendations, 16 possible champions and absence of a necessary champion remain unchanged. The original CSV, weights, anchors and manuscript are preserved.

## What the new evidence supplies

The [FSO duration workbook](https://statistik.admin.ch/de/asset/36351624), released in January 2026 with 2024 reference data, supplies five national work-journey duration bins and a separate unknown-duration population. The archived selection retains weighted counts, known-duration shares and reported VI columns without rounding or changing the observation year. Switzerland-wide resident workers are not Zurich greater-city incoming/internal commuters or upper-secondary pupils. Its frequencies therefore do not tighten the city travel interval. Source and deterministic extraction: `research/2026-a/data/current_swiss_duration2024/`.

The [Zürich Bildungsplanung request page](https://www.zh.ch/de/bildungsdirektion/generalsekretariat-der-bildungsdirektion/bildungsplanung/anfragen-an-die-bildungsplanung.html) explicitly offers tailored learner and school-staff statistics. The archived SSP profile lists school identifier, personnel category, workload, full-time basis and school type at activity level; its published regionalisation is cantons. These fields make a targeted request possible, while leaving city and stage allocation unproven. `research/2026-a/data/current_zurich_staff_request/README.md` sets out an **unsent** request for same-year upper-secondary teaching FTE and corresponding pupils in schools located in Stadt Zürich, including provider coverage, mixed stages, cross-city records and withheld/unallocated data. No email has been sent and no requested aggregate has been received.

The bounded Dublin search returned the previously audited ETB/county post-primary payroll report. Newer school-level pupil workbooks do not resolve the missing city-stage teaching denominator. The route index now records this near-match against its existing V161 scope audit, preventing a later iteration from treating its FTE figures as a new compatible city ratio.
