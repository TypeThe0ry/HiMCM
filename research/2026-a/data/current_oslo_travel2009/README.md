# Oslo 2009 travel source reconciliation

Eurostat's current `urb_ctran` response contains a matching **31.0-minute average journey to work** for 2009, indicator `TT1019V`, city code `NO001C`. The response labels this geography **Oslo (greater city)**. This is a value-and-year match to the supplied Oslo entry and a plausible source lead; it does not prove that the supplied CSV was derived from this record, identify the precise 2009 geographic definition or study population, or provide a duration distribution. The current response has no observation status field; its dataset `updated` timestamp is 2026-11-04, not an observation date. The supplied value, 2009 year and stale flag remain unchanged. A general journey-to-work mean is not a student school-trip distribution.

A separate 2009 Norwegian National Travel Survey report (TØI report 1130/2011, printed p. 43, Table 7.2) reports **26 minutes per work trip for Oslo residents**, and 30 minutes for residents of the surrounding municipalities. These are separate group means, not the 31-minute greater-city observation or evidence that the populations match. This table is not a duration distribution, and it does not justify transferring bins to the CSV value. The national work-trip factsheet and report are captured for review; neither supplies the matched distribution required for an exact-information analysis. No automatic replacement or model change follows from either source.

## Reproducibility and scope

- `eurostat_NO001C_2009.json` preserves the original response from [Eurostat's Oslo 2009 city transport API](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/api/dissemination/statistics/1.0/data/urb_ctran?lang=EN&cities=NO001C&time=2009). Indicator index 6 has value 31.0; `eurostat_metadata.json` records request URL, status, timestamp, headers, bytes and SHA-256. The full response also includes other indicators; matching one record cannot establish the CSV's provenance chain.
- `toi_2009_key_report.pdf` is the [original TØI report](https://www.toi.no/getfile.php/1316282-1299139177/Publikasjoner/T%C3%98I%20rapporter/2011/1130-2011/1130-2011-el.pdf); `report.txt` is a derived `pdftotext -layout` transcription. `toi_2009_commute_factsheet.pdf` is the [TØI work-trip factsheet](https://www.toi.no/getfile.php/1324284-1344948842/mmarkiv/Forskningsprogram/RVU/Faktaark-RVU%202009-Arbeidsreiser.pdf). Their adjacent metadata files record HTTP status, retrieval time and original-byte hashes. These are research reporting sources, not an asserted provenance match for 31 minutes.
- The search covered the TØI 2009 survey route and the Eurostat Urban Audit route. No source found here links a 2009 travel-time distribution to the exact `NO001C` 31-minute population. V203's exact-information experiment therefore remains hypothetical, not a measured distribution or a conclusion about student travel.
