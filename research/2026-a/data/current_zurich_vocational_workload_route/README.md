# Zurich vocational schools: workload-source investigation

No actual teaching FTE or teaching-lesson total was established for a compatible city-school upper-secondary subset. **New workload admissions: 0.** The available school records distinguish vocational pupils from teaching staff but do not supply their actual teaching workload, a prerequisite for a conservative lower bound. Teacher headcounts are not converted to FTE.

The [Allgemeine Berufsschule Zürich (ABZH) 2023/24 annual report](https://www.a-b-z.ch/resources/Jahresbericht_2024_VIEW1.pdf), PDF page 36, lists 106 teachers, 2,261 initial-vocational-training learners, 78 continuing-education learners, and 137 classes. These are published counts as displayed, with no claimed rounding convention or date within the school year. They are not a stage-allocated FTE measurement; the report does not give a validated actual FTE total or full-time workload distribution for these people. It concerns school year 2023/24, not the previously investigated 2024/25 pupil series. The [school’s facts and reports page](https://www.abzh.ch/ueber-uns/zahlen-und-fakten) identifies the school and a Zürich address. Its linked [2025/26 facts PDF](https://www.abzh.ch/resources/ABZH-in-Zahlen-25-261.pdf) separately reports 101 teachers and 2,452 learners; it also contains no identified actual teaching FTE, and is a different school year. The stage, locations and provider containment for any specific target count would still have to be established, as would a matching reference date and duties.

Visual review of the two numeric pages confirms the teacher labels refer to people. The 2025/26 profile displays ages 15–53 and marks its training list as excluding continuing education; its learner count is not a 14–18 age sample. The published numbers remain separate by year and population.

The [Baugewerbliche Berufsschule Zürich report](https://www.bbzh.ch/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/2024-Jahresbericht-2023-2024.pdf) covers August 2023–July 2024 and describes its vocational school activities. Text extraction of all 56 pages did not reveal an actual teaching FTE or a school-wide measured teaching-lesson total. This is a finding about the retrieved report, not proof no such data exist elsewhere. The BBZH portrait page returned HTTP 403 in direct retrieval and is preserved as a failed response, not evidence. A 2025 [cantonal government position-plan decision](https://www.zh.ch/bin/zhweb/publish/regierungsratsbeschluss-unterlagen./2025/169/RRB-2025-0169.pdf) for Berufsschule für Detailhandel und Pharmazie Zürich lists leadership, administrative, and other positions and discusses additions to administration: an approved position plan is not a measurement of actual teaching FTE. It therefore does not fill the missing gate.

A conservative bound could use several **disjoint** actual city-school teaching-workload subsets, provided their duties, school locations, upper-secondary activities and year are contained in the intended population and any rounding is outward. This investigation does not establish such a subset or prove that the earlier 63,925 cantonal pupil total is a compatible upper bound. No pupil-to-teacher ratio or model score is changed; no school or agency was contacted.

The checked pages and reports are primary school or cantonal documents; they do not certify completeness across all possible sources.
