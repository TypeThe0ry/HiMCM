# Nairobi 2020: denominator definition and coverage resolved

The [official booklet](https://www.education.go.ke/sites/default/files/Docs/The%20Basic%20Education%20Statistical%20Booklet%202020%20%281%29.pdf) now exposes full table headers through the web reader. Form 1–4 enrollment is 76,636 public plus 30,329 private, totaling 106,965. Teachers are 2,660 TSC, 706 public BOM and 2,099 private. Published PTR22 uses 4,759 teachers, excluding BOM; it is not the ratio including all three reported categories.

Methodology page7 acknowledges private-institution coverage gaps and interpolation/extrapolation for missing inputs. The reported 90% age-specific coverage is not a city enrollment-coverage bound. Consequently these historical tabulations do not justify narrowing the full-city interval.
