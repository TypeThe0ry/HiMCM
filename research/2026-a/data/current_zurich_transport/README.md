# Zurich public transport: official inventory candidate

**Admissible increase to V202: 0.** The cantonal GIS WFS is a useful cross-operator candidate, but this snapshot does not yet establish a physical-location census comparable with the 2025 VBZ aggregate of 446 stops on city territory. Do not add its records to the VBZ count or substitute its raw count.

The [city catalogue](https://data.stadt-zuerich.ch/dataset/ktzh_haltestellen_des_oeffentlichen_verkehrs___ogd_) describes a cantonal public transport stop dataset assembled with ZVV, regional operators and others, updated by VBZ, and dated 14 December 2025. Its [WFS](https://maps.zh.ch/wfs/HaltestellenZHWFS?SERVICE=WFS&VERSION=2.0.0&REQUEST=GetCapabilities) provides location, name, identifier and line fields. The original response and its retrieval time, HTTP status and SHA-256 are recorded here. The service returned 3,004 features across its full extent, including outside Zürich city. Exactly 479 records have `cname` equal to `Zürich`; that field has not been verified as an official municipality boundary test, and 17 of those records have no lines listed. Two records share the name `Zürich HB`; the rail `Zürich Stadelhofen` and surface `Zürich, Bahnhof Stadelhofen` also have separate IDs. These examples require explicit location and service eligibility reconciliation before any physical-stop count can be used. No claim is made that all such pairs are necessarily the same physical stop under the VBZ definition.

The [national service-point data](https://data.opentransportdata.swiss/en/dataset/service-point-v2) includes stops, freight points and operational points. Its [official documentation](https://opentransportdata.swiss/de/cookbook/masterdata-cookbook/servicepoints/) says some parent identifiers refer to separate operational stops within one commercial stop. Consequently, unique SLOIDs alone do not demonstrate unique physical locations. A direct national CSV download attempt returned HTTP 404; no response was treated as data.

The 2025 VBZ value of 446 and original population of 440,935 remain unchanged. No result here establishes youth access, frequency, or network coverage. Reproduce the descriptive checks with `python3 research/2026-a/data/current_zurich_transport/check.py`; it verifies the original response hash and does not modify the snapshot.
