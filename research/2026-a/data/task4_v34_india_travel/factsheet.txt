A. ABOUT THE SURVEY
Household Travel Survey is an essential tool for gathering detailed
information about the travel behaviour of individuals within a given
household
This factsheet consists of major findings from the maiden all-India National
Household Travel Survey (NHTS), conducted during July, 2025 - June, 2026. The survey
was designed to provide valuable insights into how people move, the modes of
transport they use, trip purposes, travel frequency, and other related factors.
Information about individuals' public transport usage can be utilized to identify gaps
in the current transit system, optimize routes, and adjust schedules. This data can be
used for informed decision making about where new transit routes should be
established and how to improve accessibility to underserved areas.



Sampling Design & Sample Size
A stratified two stage sampling design was adopted in NHTS. The first stage units
(FSU) were villages/urban frame survey (UFS) blocks/sub-units (SUs) as per the
situation. The ultimate stage units (USU) were households. From each selected FSU,
20 households were selected with Simple Random Sampling Without Replacement
(SRSWOR). Information on regular travel pattern for education, employment and
other purposes, along with overnight trips were collected from all members of age 6
years and above of the selected households.


Estimates presented in this Fact Sheet are based on 24,651 FSUs spread across rural
and urban areas of India. Appendix-A consists of tables (hyperlinked) of State/UT level
estimates, while Appendix-B gives Relative Standard Error (RSE) of some major
indicators obtained from NHTS (hyperlinked).

 B. Salient Features
 Travel, for the purpose of NHTS, is defined as any movement having a
 distance of one kilometer (km) or more
 In NHTS, information was collected on some usual characteristics and other aspects
 of within travel-day trips undertaken during applicable travel reference period, e.g.,
 travel to educational institution by student, travel to workplace by employed
 household members and, travel for any other purpose during the travel reference
 period.
 Any travel with a distance of less than one km has not been considered as ‘travel.’


 Exclusion
 The following types of travel, even if comprising of a distance of 1
 km or more, has been excluded from the purview of this survey:
  a. Travels which are intrinsic to employment activity, e.g., travels of vendors,
  rickshaw pullers, taxi driver, bus driver, auto driver etc. while at work.
  b. Short journeys by walk or cycle solely for the purpose of exercising, starting and
  ending at same location, with brief stopovers, e.g., jogging, morning/ evening walk,
  walking the pet etc.
  c. Journeys performed as a sub-part of amusement or for adventure e.g., jungle
  safari or, toy train ride with same origin and destination




 Trip
 For any travel performed by a household member, each movement from ‘origin’ to
 ‘destination’ without any stopover has been counted as one ‘trip’. For the purpose of
 NHTS, onward trip and return trip has been counted as two separate trips. A trip is
 uniquely specified by its purpose, origin and destination.
 It may be mentioned that ‘orgin’ of any trip reported by the hosuehold member(s)
 may or may not be the residence of the household.

 Primary Modes of Trip
 Mode of travel is the means by which the travel has been performed. If a trip was
 performed using more than one mode of transport, primary mode for that trip was
 taken as the mode by which longest distance was traversed in the trip. If a trip was
 performed by using a single mode of transport, that was decisively the primary
 mode. Coverage of some of the primary modes of transport presented in this Fact
 Sheet are given below:

 Two-wheeler includes personal two-wheeler, shared two-wheeler (of
 non- household member), ride-hail app or, rental two-wheeler

             Car includes personal car, shared car, employer-provided car, ride-hail
             app taxi, rental or hired car

 Auto-rickshaw also includes e-rickshaw

             Bus includes public bus, private (chartered) bus, school/company bus
             and Inter-city bus

         Train/monorail/tram includes Metro rail, Suburban (local) rail,
                                                      Monorail and Tram


 Cost of Trips
 In NHTS, information was collected on cost of trip for certain kind of travels. Cost of
 trip includes the expenditure borne by the household for trip-related expenses only.
 For example, if a trip is performed by own vehicle, ‘cost of trip’ may include
 components like fuel cost, cost of hiring a driver and toll tax, if these are borne by
 the household. Maintenance cost of vehicle, road tax etc. are not included in cost of
 trip.
 If multiple members participated in a trip for different purposes, cost of the trip was
 apportioned among the members and recorded against appropriate item(s) in the
 Schedule


Overnight Trip
A trip that kept a member away from his/her residence for more than 24 hours has
been considered as overnight trip. This will include the travel time as well as
duration of stay, if any, during the trip.
 Exclusions:
 i. Trips of lengths less than 50 km
 ii. Trips to regular workplace with extended periods away from
 residence. e.g., extended emergency duty hours for a doctor

 E. Overnight Trips
 As mentioned earlier, a trip of 50 km or more, that kept a member away from their
 residence for more than 24 hours, was considered as overnight trip in NHTS.
 Onward journey and return journey of an overnight trip was counted and enumerated
 separately. All overnight trips completed (and not necessarily started) during a travel
 reference period of last 28 travel-days prior to the date of the survey were considered
 for recording. Only those overnight trips were considered for which both origin and
 destination are within the geographical limits of India.

 Members from 11.3% households performed overnight trip during the reference period
 of 28 travel days. 10.3% households from rural areas and 13.1% households from urban
 areas performed overnight trips during the same reference period
