                              Concepts and Definitions
Enrolment :
Enrolment is defined as the number of individuals officially registered in a given
educational programme, or stage or module thereof, regardless of age.

Class size :
The actual number of students assigned to a specific classroom.

Teacher Student Ratio (TSR) :
Proportion of teachers relative to the number of students, calculated by dividing total
students by total teachers.
Example: Suppose a school has 500 students and 20 teachers.
       Total Number of Students
TSR =
       Total Number of Teachers
       500
    = 20
    = 25
The TSR is 25:1, meaning there are 25 students for every one teacher.

Urban Area :
Refer to the area covered by Municipalities, Upazila Head Quarters, District Head
Quarters and City Corporations in the country.

City Corporation :
City Corporation is a local government body responsible for administering and managing
a large city. It provides urban services such as waste management, road maintenance,
street lighting, public health, water supply coordination, and urban planning.
Rural Area :
Rural refers to areas located outside towns and cities, usually characterized by villages,
low population density, agricultural activities, and limited urban facilities.

Gender Parity Index (GPI) :
Gender Parity index is a socioeconomic indicator that measures the relative access to
education (or other development indicators) between females and males. It is widely used
by UNESCO Institute for Statistics (UIS), World Bank, BANBEIS (Bangladesh), and
others to track gender equality in education.

The GPI is the ratio of the female value to the male value of a given education indicator
(e.g., enrolment ratio, literacy rate, or completion rate).
    • GPI = 1 indicates perfect gender parity.
    • GPI < 1 indicates disparity in favor of males (girls/women have lower access).
    • GPI > 1 indicates disparity in favor of females.

Aims and objectives of educational survey 2025
The aims of the survey 2025 are;

       data collected through the surveys and to simultaneously make both the data providers and users
       aware of the importance of facts and figures.

       filling up the questionnaires and also use of facts and figures.

    The specific objectives of the Survey 2025 are;
