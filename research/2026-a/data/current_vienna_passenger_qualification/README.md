# Vienna passenger catalogue qualifies the scheduled-supply candidate

The operator's passenger timetable page advertises every one of the88 routes used by the378 GTFS station-area witnesses. This supplies the missing public-passenger nature evidence alongside the existing service-day calendar, regular stop fields and native DIVA crosswalk. The resulting measure is a dated, source-defined **scheduled public transport facility-supply subset**. As in the retained Vienna source policy, it does not require zero fares, no reservations, universal student access or proof that every scheduled trip actually ran.

Two original HTTP200 pages are retained, requested at `www.wienerlinien.at/fahrplaene` and the operator's `tramwm` alias. Each has93 A-route entries representing89 route names. Their parsed fields agree after normalising only the PDF-link hostname; the raw bodies and original links remain distinct. The entry's `Stand` date is a document label, not an inferred service effective date. The receipts preserve requested/final URLs, UTC, type, bytes and SHA256.

Nine witness routes have additional catalogue conditions:43A/92A winter tables;56A/58A Maxingstraße detours;74A a direction-specific detour;73A weekday/weekend versions;41A/86A/89A both citybus and callbus entries. The10November2026 service day is Tuesday, but the73A `Stand:10.11.2026` label alone neither proves nor disproves that day's GTFS applicability. These headings identify conditions to retain. They do not state a dated stop cancellation contradicting an individual witness.

Fourteen linked document requests returned HTTP200 **HTML challenge responses**, each118,395 bytes, rather than PDFs. All14 have distinct hashes and are retained once each with `PDF_magic=false` and `admitted_PDF=false` in `document_attempts.json`. No challenge script was executed; no PDF was recovered or retried. The HTML index supports route nature; it is not a claim to have read the linked timetables or verified their detailed conditions.

The [qualification results](../../results/current_vienna_passenger_qualification/README.md) preserve all route headings and36 selected witnesses belonging to the nine condition-bearing routes. Withholding those36 gives a conservative342-group check. A withheld selected witness may have an alternative ordinary witness; no such rematching is pretended, and withholding is not evidence that a station or public service is absent. Reservations and other access conditions belong in availability interpretation, not an invented redefinition of the retained facility-supply indicator.

The prior [378-group structural package](../current_vienna_gtfs/README.md) stays frozen with its original pending-qualification status. This successful route-nature review is a separate addition, not a retroactive rewrite. Primary activation, controlled reruns and paper delivery remain separate steps. Original CSV, population, weights, anchors and intervals are preserved; no agency was contacted.
