# V194 Zurich: 2025 canton school-workforce release

The new 2025/2026 canton pocket statistics provides a dated teaching-workload observation, separate from the annual payroll measure examined in V175. Physical PDF page 15 (printed page 28) gives public-school Mittelschule teaching workload **1771.6 VZE in 2025**, average employment fraction **63.0%**, and **2812 appointments**. The last quantity counts appointments, not unique teachers or FTE. The school category includes gymnasia, HMS, IMS and FMS. The physical page was rendered and visually inspected; the text extraction matches these three entries.

The report is canton-wide and public-school scoped. It does not supply the missing city private-provider teaching denominator, a matched city pupil population, or a one-sided numerical rounding envelope. It therefore supplies neither a city PTR nor an upper bound covering all city teaching activity. No ratio is calculated and no source is admitted. The active V192 science and V193 paper remain unchanged; actual scoring-interval reduction is zero.

The adjacent printed page 29 has a different measure: Mittelschulen administrative reporting of 2227 VZE for financial year 2025. Its note defines work volume over the accounting year; it is not substituted for the teaching census workload or a point-in-time denominator. The report title is 2025/2026; the observed table year is 2025, the editorial data status is 28 May 2026, and the landing page publication date is July 2026. These dates are retained separately.
