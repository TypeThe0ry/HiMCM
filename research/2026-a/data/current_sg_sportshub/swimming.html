A Splash in the Pool

Featuring a 10-lane Competition Pool and an 8-lane Training Pool, the 3,000-seat OCBC Aquatic Centre caters to athletes and swimming enthusiasts alike. Additional temporary seating can expand the seating capacity to 6,000 seats, enabling it to host regional and international aquatic competitions and community events.


Whether you swim for fitness or fun or gold medals, there are lessons, workouts and programmes to enhance your time in the water.

Kids and adults can learn to swim or enjoy aqua fit sessions, diving classes, and artistic swimming. For competitive swimmers, there is lap swimming and elite coaching.

For those who swim for fitness, stress relief, or mindfulness, our aqua activities will challenge, invigorate and thrill.

To ensure the safety of our patrons, the minimum age for using the Competition Pool is 16* years old.
*refers to the age attained after the birthday.
