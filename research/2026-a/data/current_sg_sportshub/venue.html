OCBC Aquatic Centre

Venues & Amenities

Take a refreshing swim at the sheltered swimming pools that meet the World Aquatics​ standards.

About The Venue

Details overview

Share
  1. About
  2. Getting There

A world-class aquatic facility, the OCBC Aquatic Centre is built in accordance with the guidelines of the World Aquatics.

 

Featuring a 50m x 10 lane competition pool (3m deep), a 50m x 8 lane training pool (1.35 - 2m deep) and a 25m wide diving pool/multi-purpose pool (5m deep), this facility caters to both the general public and elite athlete training, with the competition and training pools open for public swimming.

 

Home to Singapore Aquatics, it was the host venue for the 28th SEA Games aquatics discipline, where Joseph Schooling set nine Games records and Singapore Synchronised Swimming Team won their first gold medal in the sport.

 

More recently, the OCBC Aquatic Centre hosted thrilling world-class water polo action at the World Aquatics Championships, culminating in Spain being crowned men's champions and Greece claiming the women's title. It also hosted the Toyota World Para Swimming Championships for the first time in Singapore. It showcased extraordinary commitment and incredible athleticism across 172 swimming events, including backstroke, breaststroke and freestyle races, as well as the dynamic individual and mixed medleys.

VENUE ENHANCEMENT WORKS

The Competition Pool at OCBC Aquatic Centre will undergo enhancement works and will be closed from 5 to 28 December 2026.

During this period, Training Pool availability will be limited. Please refer to the Pool Schedule for the latest operating hours and availability before planning your visit.

We apologise for any inconvenience caused and appreciate your understanding.
