# V48 Santiago: a new official source, no unsupported narrowing

The March 2026 DTPM **Reporte de Movilidad 2025** was downloaded directly from the official publisher. This is a newly inspected source, not a re-download of the EOD2012 mirrors. `source_audit.json` records its URL, byte count and SHA-256. `mobility_report2025.txt` is the local `pdftotext -layout` extraction of the archived original PDF.

The 2025 household survey has 412 valid records across 34 Gran Santiago communes; 46 are in the published 12–20 age group (pp. 7–8). The age bin cannot isolate current high-school students aged 14–18. The education table describes education attained, so its 158 Media observations must not be treated as 158 current secondary students. This finding concerns EM2025 and does not establish the meaning of the separate EOD2012 Estudios/Curso fields.

The final results fuse EM2025, EOD2012 and mobile-phone data. The study-purpose category includes every education level (p. 13). These aggregate results therefore cannot replace high-school-specific journey-time observations. No released respondent microdata link was located in this bounded official-site search; this does not establish that none exist.

**Model consequence:** no Santiago input, missing-data interval or possible-winner set is narrowed by this report. The EOD2012 current-stage questionnaire gate remains open. The useful improvement is source discrimination: a 2025 report is now verified and archived, while its mixed-year and all-stage aggregates are explicitly prevented from becoming fabricated new high-school measurements.

The report recommends increasing the sample to 1,200 households and improving interview scheduling and short-trip measurement (p. 23). It is useful context for future collection, but provides no defensible numerical reduction of the present uncertainty.

Files here are research source material; no manuscript or model-core edits were made by this audit.
