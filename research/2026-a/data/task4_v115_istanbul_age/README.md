# V115 Istanbul: age and spatial-access source audit

Two new primary originals were obtained. Neither narrows the existing travel interval [0,1].

Devkota’s 2010 Twente thesis covers four districts. Its reported proximity result is modeled access to the nearest school, not travel to the school a student actually attends. The age14–17 demand is interpolated from broader age bands. PDF pages36,40,47,63 establish the geography, interview design, modeling assumptions and stated absence of observed travel behavior. It is useful for distinguishing facility location from student access, not for substituting a duration statistic.

Özdoğru’s article (published2021; fieldwork year unverified) covers one convenience sample of180 grade10/11 students aged15–18. Its questionnaire concerns out-of-school activities, not school journeys. Neither city-wide age comparisons nor student preference weights can be inferred from it. In particular, no14-year-old comparison group exists.

`manifest.json` preserves original URLs and hashes. `audit.py` verifies source bytes and decisive text. `audit.json` records the non-admission. Model cells, weights, and registry are unchanged.

Publication year is not the observation year. The article reports2015 permission dates and an earlier2018 presentation; neither establishes the fieldwork date, so no2015/2021 data vintage is assigned.
