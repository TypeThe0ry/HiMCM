Overview

The Department of Education and Youth adhere to the core standards reflected in the Civil
Service Code of Standards and Behaviours, the Code of Ethics, and the Civil Service Renewal
Plan. These standards include the important principal of quality, along with other core values
such as objectivity, impartiality, and accountability.

Introduction

Education statistics in the area of learners (entrants, enrolments, graduates), teachers and
expenditure are of significant interest to the public and are vital for the performance and
management of the education system in Ireland. Trust in these statistics is vital for good
governance of the sector and is critical for evidence-based policymaking. Trust in the quality
and impartiality of these statistics is of paramount importance and this document outlines
how the Department of Education and Youth adhere to the core principals identified in the
Irish Statistical System Code of Practice, such as:

       •   Commitment to quality;
       •   Professional independence;
       •   Statistical Confidentiality;
       •   Timeliness and punctuality; and
       •   Accessibility and clarity.


General Information

The Department of Education and Youth participate in the collection, collation, analysis, and
dissemination of a wide range of education data. DEY publish a workforce data bulletin,
amongst other reports, annually in January.

The reference year for data in this publication is the current school year, for example the
report published in January 2026 is for the school year 2025/26.

The statistical information published in the workforce data bulletin includes:

   •   Teacher payroll and allocation counts and summaries for Primary & Post Primary
   •   Data on substitutes and leave
   •   Irish Medium Education (IME) teachers
   •   Special Education Teachers and Special Needs Assistants
Organisational Unit Responsible

Statistics section is responsible for collating, validating, and compiling into the required
format required by the DEY the following data: data on teachers at primary and post primary
level and SNAs at primary and post primary level, including contracted, full time, part time
and substitute staff.

The statistics section comprises of one senior statistician and six statisticians.

Periodicity

The workforce data bulletin is published annually in January on the Department of Education
and Youth website.

Users

The statistics published in the workforce data bulletin are designed with the objective of
being both informative and accessible to a wide readership. The publications are used by a
broad range of stakeholders, including:

        •     Members of the public
        •     Researchers and research bodies
        •     Political parties and public representatives
        •     Education stakeholders such as staff representative bodies, school management
              bodies, various other education agencies.
        •     Other Government Departments / Public Service
        •     Internal stakeholders such as teacher supply, school governance, forward
              planning, and teacher allocations.
        •     Journalists and other media providers


Data Sources

   •    Information on teachers and SNAs is proved by the relevant payroll departments,
        Allocations Team, The Teaching Council, and the Special Education department.
   •    Information on schools is obtained from a central database called the Primary Online
        Database and the Post Primary Online Database.
Quality Principle

The core function of the statistics section within the Department is to produce statistics that
are accurate, reliable, coherent, and comparable. The statistics section is committed to being
objective in its compilation and analysis of the data it produces and aims to disseminate

statistics in a clear, accessible, and timely manner. The following section outlines the
statistics section approach to delivering on its commitment to the Irish Statistical System
Code of Practice quality principles in the production of statistics.

       Relevance

       The statistics compiled by statistics section are compiled in timely manner for annual
       publication.

       Accuracy and Reliability

       There is a high reputational benefit for the Department of Education in ensuring the
       accuracy and reliability of the education statistics. The data is compiled by
       professional statisticians who are committed to upholding the principles of accuracy
       and reliability. The data published in the workforce data bulletin is used and
       referenced by stakeholders right across the education system and is trusted by all. In
       compiling the data statistics section perform multiple layers of check and validations
       to ensure the accuracy of the data.

       Timeliness and Punctuality

       The workforce data bulletin is published annually in January.

       Coherence and Comparability
       All the data provided for publication is drawn from administrative sources that are
       used to manage the education system and accordingly is regarded as accurate and
       reliable.

       The data is checked against other published statistics such as external surveys,
       detailed census statistics, CSO survey results in the area of education.

       Change over time is monitored carefully to ensure coherence with previous data
       while all significant changes are examined carefully to maintain accuracy.
       Accessibility and Clarity

       Publications are available in PDF form, with relevant csv files, via a link on the
       Department’s web site.

       The statistical tables upon which reports are based, are accessible via links from the
       Department’s website to PX stat tables on the CSO web site.



Production of Statistics

Overview

The following section outlines current processes for the production of the workforce data
bulletin.

Collect, Process & Analyse Phase



Statistics section are responsible for analysing and validating each component of the tables
included, including the overall teacher numbers.

Statistics section extract workforce data from the Department’s payroll systems. The data is
analysed, and teacher counts are grouped by schools and county.

Data on allocations is provided by the department’s allocation teams in school governance
and special education.

Data on schools is extracted from the Department’s Primary Online Database (POD) and Post
Primary Online Database (PPOD).
Leave and Substitute teacher data is extracted from the Department’s Online Leave & Claims
System.



The data is summarised into the required format for the report and grouped by county and
national level.

Once the data is compiled, checked, and verified the report template is completed for
publication.
Dissemination Phase

This phase manages the publication of the workforce data bulletin on the department’s
website in January.

The bulletin is published on the Department’s website as a pdf document, with relevant data
tables as csv files.

The statistical tables upon which reports are based, are accessible via links from the
Department’s website to PX stat tables on the CSO web site.



Revisions

As quality is reviewed on an ongoing basis revision can occur. Once the need for a revision is
identified the publication is removed from the Department’s website and the CSO website,
the revision is made and when appropriate a note is added.

Confidentiality

Statistics section collect a significant amount of data which is used to develop and produce
the tables required for the workforce data bulletin. All data collected by the statistics section
is treated as strictly confidential.

All education statistics are compiled by professional statisticians. These statisticians are
recruited in the same manner as all other professional statisticians working for the CSO, they
undergo the same training, and work to the same principles and standards. Professional staff
are all signed up as Officers of Statistics. The unit is headed up by a senior statistician, also
professionally recruited, on temporary (5 year) secondment to the department.
There are data sharing agreements in place with all agencies who share data with us and the
section work in full compliance with GDPR obligations. We do not make any microdata available
to users. All confidential microdata is stored in a secure folder on the network / data hub and
access is strictly limited to statisticians in the section along with economic research and
evaluation staff in the department.

Staff must adhere to the central policies such as:

    •   Civil Service Code of Standards and Behaviours
    •   Official Secrets Act
    •   Code of Ethics in Public Office
