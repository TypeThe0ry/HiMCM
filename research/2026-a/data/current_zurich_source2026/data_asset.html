City Statistics: Ausgewählte Variablen nach Städten und Agglomerationen 2020 (in German)

Dieses Dataset präsentiert jährliche Daten für eine Auswahl von Variablen in den City Statistics Städten und Agglomerationen, gemäss der Version 2020 der Agglomerationen, ab 2010.

Details

Observation period
2010-2025
Periodicity
Semiannual
Spatial division
Larger urban zones, Urban Audit: agglomeration (Swiss perimeter), Urban Audit: core city (Swiss perimeter), Urban Audit: core city and belt (Swiss perimeter)

About us

The Federal Statistical Office (FSO) is the Confederation’s centre of expertise for creating value from data and for official statistics. It promotes data interoperability and, through its data infrastructure and services as well as its statistical information, lays the foundations for a modern public administration and informed decision-making.
