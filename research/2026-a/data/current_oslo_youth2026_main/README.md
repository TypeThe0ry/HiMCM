## Population and methods

The January–April2026 survey was conducted in85 lower- and upper-secondary schools. Printed78 (physical80) reports27472 pupils who logged in and answered at least one question, out of37308 pupils at participating schools:74% overall,79% lower-secondary and68% upper-secondary response. The stage participant counts15180 and12292 sum to27472. These are participation counts, not the denominators of the selected items or the final analysis sample.

The report describes excluding566 scant questionnaires and132 respondents aged20 or above; we do not infer an exact analysis sample by subtracting these numbers. Responses are weighted to2018 school-grade and district composition, not equally across the six grades and not identically to the prior lower-stage district report's stated three-grade balancing. Item response varies; selected item/grade denominators were not obtained.

This is a voluntary school survey. Apprentices and young people who have left school are outside its sample. High absence, specialised school settings and language/cognitive barriers affect inclusion. The main report also contains participants who report living outside Oslo or do not answer district; grade charts are therefore not silently relabelled as an all-resident adolescent census. The evidence supports reported cross-sectional participation and reasons, not exact-age14/18 preferences, longitudinal dropout, causal effects, individually verified access, or latent demand for pools.
