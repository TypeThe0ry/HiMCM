Other Data Forms Data Form D Appendices Appendix B1 Appendix D1/D2 Appendix E Appendix H Appendix M Appendix O1 Appendix O2 Appendix Q Appendix R Error and Warnings Error Confirmation Warning Message Explanation 2024-25 FS Printable Instructions English English Français Light Dark System Scroll to top Enrolment The forms in this section are designed to collect pupil enrolment for pupils of the board and other pupils. The data that is input here feeds into the following reports:
- Schedule 12: Enrolment - Continuing Education and Other Programs
- Schedule 13-1: Day School Enrolment - Pupils of the Board
- Schedule 13-2: Day School Enrolment - Other Pupils
- Schedule 13-3: Day School Enrolment - ADE
- Schedule 13-4: Prior Year Amounts For pupils of the board under age 21, enrolment must be input first on the School Level Data form. Once this is complete, board-level input is required on the Sch 12 & 13 Enrolment form to report the number of full-time and part-time pupils (including high-credit), and the FTE of high-credit full-time and part-time pupils. For other pupils, pupils of the board aged 21 years and over, and continuing education and summer school, all input occurs on the Sch 12 & 13 Enrolment form. Please note that in the Financial Statements cycle, the majority of Schedule 13 data (and all pupil enrolment that is reported on the School Level Data form) is loaded from OnSIS.
