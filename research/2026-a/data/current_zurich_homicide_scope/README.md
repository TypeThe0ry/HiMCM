# Zurich 2024 homicide: population and source scope

The original 0.14 per 100,000 remains unlinked to an established source, numerator and denominator. This check adds the official city population release and the municipal police-statistics catalogue; it does not replace any input or establish a victim rate.

- The [city release](https://www.stadt-zuerich.ch/de/aktuell/medienmitteilungen/2025/02/fast-450000-einwohnerinnen-in-der-stadt-zuerich.html) reports 448,664 residents at the end of 2024. Its change of 1,582 implies 447,082 at the end of 2023 in the same municipal series. These are city resident counts, not a demonstrated match to the original 2025 population of 440,935 or the PKS federal permanent-population series.
- The existing PKS 2025 city table reports one completed homicide offence (Swiss Criminal Code articles 111–113/116) for 2024. The archived report defines a recorded offence independently of the number of injured persons. The unit is an offence, not an independently verified victim count. See `../task4_v144_homicide/scope_audit.json` and its original PDF archive `../task4_v144_homicide/zurich2025_original.pdf.gz` (archive SHA-256 `5a745a42932435300b524805e4b3101dcf5af4c0933c07f41e27f28d5c2c029d`), pages 117–118. The decompressed original PDF SHA-256 is `cff4b7fad2544394ee3c764d0410ce1a4b985a162099955d3ad45386ac7832af`.
- The [municipal PKS catalogue](https://data.stadt-zuerich.ch/dataset/ktzh_pks_straftaten_tatbestandgruppe_gemeinden_stadtkreise) describes police-registered offences and calculates its published frequency per 1,000 inhabitants using the previous year-end federal permanent population. It records cases when the first police report is completed; it does not necessarily use the year of the act. Its own attribute description of Ausgangsjahr says year of the act, so that label is not sufficient to resolve the time basis without the PKS definition. The linked response is preserved as `municipal_pks_grouped_offences.csv`: its 8,190 records span 2009–2022 and its 13 offence groups have no homicide-specific category; catalogue period metadata itself says through 2023. Neither establishes a 2024 homicide rate.

For scale only, combining the single registered completed offence with 448,664 municipal-register residents gives 0.2229 offences per 100,000. This arithmetic illustration is not an officially published rate, a validated source-compatible rate, or a victim rate. One divided by the original 440,935 gives 0.2268 per 100,000. Under the conditional assumptions of exactly one numerator, a rate per 100,000, and conventional rounding to two decimals, a displayed 0.14 would require a denominator of approximately 689,655–740,741; this range identifies no actual population or geography. The original 0.14 could use a different definition, numerator, or provenance, none established here.
