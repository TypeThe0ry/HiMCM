# Santiago V40: independent official trip-duration semantics

No new admissible commute observation. The V10 raw mirror, cohort filters and conditional trip-chain extraction already exist; they were reviewed rather than counted again.

New source audit: DTPM's October 2018 modelling report, section 2.2.5 (printed pp.2-49–2-50), describes EOD2012 records as complete journeys across transport modes. It identifies person, journey and stage tables and explains that start/end times are recorded for complete journeys, not individual stages. This supports interpreting journey timestamps as whole-trip timing; it does not prove any person's current high-school enrolment. The report also warns that the historical observations may diverge from later travel patterns.

The transport ministry's 2015 release independently dates interviews to July 2012–November 2013, expanded to reference year 2012, across 45 communes. A 2017 census recalibration must not be labelled a newly observed 2017 travel survey.

The original `Estudios`/`Curso` questionnaire definition remains unverified. SECTRA catalogue and MTT library requests timed out. A direct report download timed out before completion; no partial PDF is represented as an archived original. Source URLs, report locations and access outcomes are recorded in `source_audit.json`.

Therefore V10's conditional results remain conditional. No new city value, reduced missing interval, rank update or manuscript modification is warranted by this audit.
