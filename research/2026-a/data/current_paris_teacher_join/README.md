# Paris: institution keys expose three distinct denominator problems

The official DEPP exports now cover the2025 Paris teacher panel and both lycée pupil streams, extending the earlier single-school Henri-IV scope check. Exact UAI joins identify154 pupil institutions:122 general/technological (GT),59 vocational (PRO), with27 appearing in both streams.153 have a teacher record. The missing teacher record is public Lycée innovant de Paris, UAI0754401B, with117 GT pupils; it is not filled with zero.

The teacher export contains339 unique institution records for rentrée2025, including collèges as well as lycées. The pupil-matched153 records contain7,920.09 institution-assigned teacher FTE. Joining GT and PRO independently and then summing the two teacher columns would count an extra1,870.52 FTE from the27 overlapping institution keys. A FTE sum is not a count of individual teachers. The GT and PRO pupil sums are57,460 and14,167 respectively; neither stream is represented as all Paris upper-secondary provision or a census of distinct residents.

## School identifier and teaching stage are different questions

The current annuaire lycée query returns243 rows but237 unique UAIs. Six keys represent multiple rows, often separate sites; four have different post-baccalaureate flags across their rows. These are preserved as candidate sets rather than overwritten by a dictionary or multiplied into pupil/teacher rows. Among the154 pupil institutions, current flags agree on post-bac for88, agree on no post-bac for60, differ across sites for4 and are unknown for2. Absence of a post-bac flag is not a recovered2025 duty allocation.

Three pupil UAIs were absent from the lycée-filtered annuaire extract. A separate exact-key query finds all three under type EREA, not Lycée. Together the two annuaire responses have246 candidate rows and240 distinct UAIs. A type-filter miss is therefore not proof of an absent or closed school. The original lycée query remains preserved, alongside the separate three-record response.

The current lycée extract also includes45 distinct HORS CONTRAT UAIs and8 with contracts for only part of their classes. The teacher panel's [archived official metadata](../task4_v129_education/paris_teacher_metadata_original.json) defines institution-assigned FTE for public and contracted-private establishments, describes suppression below5 ETP for characteristics, and explains that hospital/medico-social institutions are outside this establishment view. It is not a demonstrated all-provider teacher denominator. The metadata date is30November2025; pupil rows are rentrée2025, while annuaire rows are a current2026 snapshot. These records do not establish an exact common-date, upper-stage allocation. Current contract/site flags are coverage diagnostics, not historical certifications.

## Originals and extraction

Seven HTTP200 JSON originals and their receipts retain exact request/final URLs, UTC, type, bytes and SHA256. Two initial pupil queries used department075 and returned empty arrays. The archived successful Henri-IV record shows75 in the pupil field, so corrected75 exports were requested once; annuaire uses075. All responses remain available, including the valid empty arrays. No missing count is inferred from the wrong-code queries. GT/PRO exports include2019–2025, but this extraction selects2025 locally and makes no time-trend claim.

The sources are the [DEPP teacher dataset](https://data.education.gouv.fr/explore/dataset/fr-en-indicateurs_personnels_etablissements2d/), [GT pupil dataset](https://data.education.gouv.fr/explore/dataset/fr-en-lycee_gt-effectifs-niveau-sexe-lv/), [vocational pupil dataset](https://data.education.gouv.fr/explore/dataset/fr-en-lycee_pro-effectifs-niveau-sexe-lv/) and [education annuaire](https://data.education.gouv.fr/explore/dataset/fr-en-annuaire-education/).
