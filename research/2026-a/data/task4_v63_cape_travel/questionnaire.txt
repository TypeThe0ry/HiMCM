           STATIC TEXT

           AIM AND USE OF THE SURVEY
           STATIC TEXT

           The aim of the GHS is to generate national indicators to measure the level of development and performance of various
           government programmes and projects. Where such indicators exist, the survey will act as a monitoring and evaluation
           tool. In the absence of indicators, the survey will provide information to identify indicators that could possibly be
           monitored in future.
           STATIC TEXT

           OBJECTIVES OF THE SURVEY
           STATIC TEXT

           1.To generate national, provincial and metropolitan municipality development indicators that will measure the life
           circumstances of South African households and individuals
           2. To monitor the performance of government departments countrywide and contribute information towards measuring
           the impact of their policies and programmes.
           3.To provide information on the need, availability and use of services provided by government.
           Thank you for agreeing to participate in this very important national initiative.
