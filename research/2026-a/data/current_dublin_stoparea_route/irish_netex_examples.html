Stop Data

Summary of mapping of Irish NaPTAN Stop data to NeTEx

For timetables references to stops:



NaPTAN
NeTEx
Rail, bus and coach  stations, StopArea ? SCHEDULED STOP POINT + STOP AREA..
On-street stops StopArea ? STOP AREA
On-street stops StopPoint ? SCHEDULED STOP POINT

Additionally, for physical stop data


NaPTAN
NeTEx
Rail, bus and coach  stations, StopArea ? STOP PLACE, and STOP PLACE ENTRANCE.
On-street stops StopArea ? STOP PLACE. If there is no NaPTAN StopArea for a StopPoint, (or pair of points)   one is created with the same name.
On-street stops StopPoint ? QUAY
  • A STOP PLACE ASSIGNMENT can be used to associate a given SCHEDULED STOP POINT with a given STOP PLACE and QUAY
