# V116: Istanbul microdata access finding

Checked 2026-11-08. No city indicator or uncertainty interval is changed by this audit.

## New evidence

The archived original ERG / Suna’nın Kızları report, *Eğitime Erişim Durumlarına Göre Türkiye’de Kız Çocuklar* (2025), analyzes Turkey Child Survey 2022 microdata. PDF page 8, printed page 13, states that the survey provides national estimates and no questionnaire asks residence at province or smaller-area level; the authors consequently cannot provide province or regional breakdowns. This is an original microdata-user methodology statement, **not an inspected official variable dictionary**.

The report describes household-member, age 0–17, and age 13–17 questionnaires. Its geographic limitation means that an approved standard microdata download cannot be assumed to permit filtering Istanbul. It does not establish that TÜİK has no confidential geographic linkage in its sampling frame or cannot prepare a custom tabulation.

## Admission decision

There is still no verified public file demonstrating all three required fields together: Istanbul-identifiable geography, the child’s current upper-secondary enrollment, and school journey duration. Age 15–17 is not a substitute for current enrollment stage, and caregiver education is not student stage. The previously found national journey-time table remains national evidence only.

No publicly inspectable official Time Use 2025 or 2014 codebook proving the same joint fields was obtained in this check. National publication geography alone does not prove that underlying microdata have no geographic identifiers. No claim about a definitive 2025 microdata release date is made.

## Practical next step

Before requesting access, obtain provider confirmation of whether an approved extract/custom tabulation can jointly identify Istanbul, current upper-secondary enrollment, and journey time, with valid survey weights and adequate sample size. Standard access approval by itself would not resolve the geographic limitation documented by the microdata users. No application, account registration, or external message was submitted. Retain the existing missing interval until qualifying city evidence is available.

Source URL, byte count, and SHA-256 are recorded in manifest.json. The archived PDF is the substantive new evidence; repeated blocked-page responses are not archived.
