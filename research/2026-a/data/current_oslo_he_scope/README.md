# Oslo higher-education count: source scope

These three official pages provide national institutional context and examples of study opportunities in Oslo. They do not identify or validate the original input's 25 institutions in Oslo. No observation is admitted to the model from this archive; the original CSV and V203 point remain unchanged.

- `nokut_institutions.html` lists four national categories: universities, specialised universities, institutionally accredited university colleges, and university colleges with accredited study programmes. A programme's accreditation is not the same as institutional accreditation. The page is a current national list, not a dated Oslo municipal inventory.
- `nokut_general_education.html` reports 49 institutions nationally for January 2026: 11 universities, nine specialised university institutions, 15 university colleges, and 14 non-institutionally-accredited colleges with approved first-degree programmes. This national figure is not an Oslo count. The page's broad opening description of 49 as accredited must be read with its explicit distinction for the 14 colleges.
- `oslo_student.html` describes higher education opportunities and lists examples. It does not provide the exact 25-entity roster or define the scope used by the original input.

The January 2026 national snapshot and the separately retrieved live institution list are not established as the same dated roster. Neither establishes a 2026 count within Oslo municipality. The original input records 25 and 34.4 per million with population 727,797; institution eligibility, distinct-entity counting, location boundary, and the exact source roster remain unverified. A postal address, campus, programme, or national listing alone cannot establish that an institution belongs in that city count. These checks do not constitute an exhaustive search for city evidence.
