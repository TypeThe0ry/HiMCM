Higher education institutions

There are four main categories of institutions providing higher education in Norway: Universities, Specialised Universities, University Colleges/Universitites of Applied Sciences and University Colleges with accredited study programmes.

Universities, Specialised Universities and University Colleges/Universitites of Applied Sciences are granted the right to offer educational provision, without having to apply to NOKUT for specific (programme) accreditation, in accordance with the powers that its institutional category implies.

  • Universities

    Universities have the right to establish programmes at all levels.

  • Specialised Universities

    Specialised Universities can accredit study programmes at bachelor’s degree level in all subject areas. Specialised Universities have accreditation for doctoral studies primarily within one subject area and can accredit new study programmes at higher degree level (master) within this subject area. The institution must apply to NOKUT for accreditation for study programmes at master's level outside the subject area in which the institution already has been granted to award a doctorate.

  • University Colleges/Universitites of Applied Sciences

    University Colleges/Universitites of Applied Sciences have the authority to accredit their own study programmes at the lower degree level (bachelor) within all subject areas. If the institution has been accredited for doctoral studies within one or more subject areas, the institution has the authority to accredit their own study programmes at the higher degree level (master) within these subject areas. In subject areas where the institution cannot award a doctorate, the institution must apply to NOKUT for accreditation of study programmes at the master's and doctoral levels.

  • University Colleges with accredited study programmes

    University Colleges with accredited study programmes (without institutional accreditation) do not have the authority to accredit their own study programmes, but must apply to NOKUT for accreditation of all new study programmes.
