 *820. To ask the Minister for Education the estimated cost of completing all currently needed
new school buildings and repairs and all buildings and repairs projected to be needed over the
next ten years. — Paul Murphy. [32967/24]

  *821. To ask the Minister for Education the estimated cost of guaranteeing the right to non-
denominational or multi-denominational education to all non-Catholic students currently
in Catholic schools through the provision of alternative non-denominational or multi-
denominational schools. — Paul Murphy. [32968/24]

  *822. To ask the Minister for Education the annual cost of State payments to private schools
including payments to staff. — Paul Murphy. [32969/24]

 *823. To ask the Minister for Education the estimated annual cost of providing special
education teachers, special needs assistants and other additional needs supports to schools on a
“trust” basis, that is in response to requests for resources from school principals based on their
assessment of school needs. — Paul Murphy. [32970/24]

 *824. To ask the Minister for Education if she will detail for all primary schools in each of
Dublin 1, Dublin 3, Dublin 7, Dublin 9 and Dublin 11, the total number of students in each
area; the total number of teachers in each area; the total number of SNAs in each area; the total
Exchequer funding in each; the average class size in each area; the pupil-teacher ratio in each
area; the number of pupils receiving free schoolbooks in each area, in tabular form; and if she
will make a statement on the matter. — Paul McAuliffe. [33193/24]

 *825. To ask the Minister for Education if she will detail for all post primary schools in each
of Dublin 1, Dublin 3, Dublin 7, Dublin 9 and Dublin 11, the total number of students in each
area; the total number of teachers in each area; the total number of SNAs in each area; the total
Exchequer funding in each; the average class size in each area; the pupil-teacher ratio in each
area; the number of pupils receiving free schoolbooks in each area, in tabular form; and if she
will make a statement on the matter. — Paul McAuliffe. [33194/24]

 *826. To ask the Minister for Education the estimated full-year and first-year cost,
respectively, of reducing the pupil teacher staffing ratio by one point at primary level; the
estimated capital cost of additional accommodation of same; the existing staffing ratios; the
revised ratios; and if she will make a statement on the matter. — Ivana Bacik. [32990/24]

 *827. To ask the Minister for Education the full-year and first-year cost, respectively, of
reducing the pupil teacher staffing ratio by one point, at post primary level; what the capital cost
of additional accommodation would be; to provide the existing staffing ratios; what the revised
ones would be; and if she will make a statement on the matter. — Ivana Bacik. [32991/24]

 *828. To ask the Minister for Education the existing pupil teacher staffing ratios or additional
allocations to each type of DEIS school; the additional cost of reducing those ratios by one point
each; and if she will make a statement on the matter. — Ivana Bacik. [32992/24]

  *829. To ask the Minister for Education the additional number of special education teachers
recruited in the 2023-2024 school year; the additional number to be recruited in the 2024-2025
school year; the extra number of special class teachers and extra number of teachers in special
schools; the projected additional recruitment needed of each in 2025-2026 to meet existing
levels of service and demographics and provide a breakdown of the cost; and if she will make a
statement on the matter. — Ivana Bacik. [32993/24]
