# V127 education: no new city PTR admitted

The current sixteen-city upper-secondary teacher-ratio cells remain unchanged. This bounded check screened Zurich's known routes and inspected two new official avenues: a Dublin parliamentary question and DUO's Dutch institution-level pupil–teacher data. Neither establishes a matched all-provider city upper-secondary numerator and denominator. `observations.json` is empty; no utility bound is proposed.

## Findings

- **Zurich:** a narrow city-FTE search returned city pupil material and the already-rejected canton/public-school staffing routes. Those sources were not downloaded again. The V124 scope finding remains applicable; this search does not prove that no suitable city source exists.
- **Dublin:** the official 23 July 2024 question paper, PDF page75, question825 [33194/24], requests pupils, teachers and PTR for Dublin1/3/7/9/11 post-primary schools. It supplies a question, not the requested results. The answer page was inaccessible in the web tool and the alternate official prelive endpoint timed out in Exa. Even a recovered answer would need its reference year, actual staffing definition, upper-secondary scope and partial postal coverage checked before a city claim. No allocated posts or national ratios were substituted.
- **Amsterdam:** DUO's official `resource_show` API metadata for `Functiemix instellingen` confirms that a BRIN institution may contain multiple campuses and that personnel are not collected at campus level. Its data include an instruction-staffing-size field (`OMVANG_FORMATIE_OP`) and a published pupil–teacher-ratio field. No independently matched city teaching-FTE denominator is inferred from these fields. The archived one-record search response contains a historical havo/vwo institution (2006), demonstrating the schema; it is not a current Amsterdam estimate. Institution address alone does not establish a complete municipality workforce, all-provider coverage or a separation of upper-secondary years. No aggregate was constructed.

Two genuinely new official avenues were opened, below the maximum of three. A Milan search returned no matched city teacher ratio and was not expanded into another data route. No additional regional proxy was pursued.
