National Stop Register (NSR)

National Stop Register (NSR) is the master database for public transport stops in Norway and is primarily used to store and redistribute detailed information regarding the infrastructure of a stop place.

The data is created and maintained by the counties (fylkeskommune) with Entur acting as the overall administrator. All stops currently in active use by public transport services are required to present and active in NSR, regardless of physical condition. The purpose of having a master database for all stops is to avoid duplication of stop data across providers and to make it easier for new operators to plan, and establish routes. The basic information stored in NSR is ID, name, relation, and position, but other data such as shelter or wheelchair accessibility is also handled.

ℹ Navigate to https://stoppested.entur.org to explore the contents of NSR.

NeTEx publications

All stop place data is exported daily to NeTEx files that are freely available under NLOD license. This is the preferred way to consume stop place data.

API

Please refer to the API page for API documentation.

Alternatively, stop places information is also available in the Geocoder-, and Journey Planner APIs.
