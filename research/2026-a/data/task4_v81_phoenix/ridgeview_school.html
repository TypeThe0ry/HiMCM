Ridgeview College Preparatory High School is proud to be part of Leona Group Schools, providing quality education across Arizona for over 25 years.

Our Mission: Ridgeview College Preparatory High School creates a tradition of academic success built on the pillars of college readiness, community service, love of learning, and self-discipline.
