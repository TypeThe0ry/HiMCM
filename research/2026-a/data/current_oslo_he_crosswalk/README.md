# Oslo organization identities and the archived NOKUT roster

Sixteen of the 28 Oslo-label ROR education records have an identity match to the archived NOKUT national institution list. Fifteen fall in institutionally accredited categories; Oslo New University College is listed under colleges with accredited study programmes **without institutional accreditation**. These matches establish named national-list correspondence; they do not establish an Oslo municipal inventory, reconstruct the submitted 25, or justify replacing a model value.

`national_roster.csv` preserves the 48 links in the archived NOKUT institution page: 11 universities, nine specialised universities, 15 institutionally accredited university colleges and 13 colleges with accredited programmes. This independently extracted roster is distinct from the other archived page's January 2026 national total of 49 (11/9/15/14). No cause for the difference or shared observation date is asserted. The source's `Universitites` heading spelling and malformed Norges Høyskole for Helsefag website URL remain as published.

`oslo_crosswalk.csv` retains all 28 ROR candidates. Identity evidence is an exact normalized match to a ROR name or a shared official website host, with each supporting ROR name/URL recorded. Only Unicode accent removal, case-folding and whitespace normalization apply to names; URL comparison removes `www.` from the hostname and does not compare substrings or parent domains. Twelve unmatched records are unresolved under these rules, not classified as ineligible. The ROR locations remain GeoNames labels rather than municipal polygon or campus coverage evidence. Multi-campus institutions and provider/branch counting rules still require verification.

Three new official pages describe different organizational roles within the education-tagged ROR candidates: Universities Norway presents its sector interest/cooperative role; the Norwegian Nobel Institute explains its Nobel Committee support/secretariat role; Forskerfabrikken describes science activities for children. These affirmative descriptions explain why a general education tag does not itself supply an accredited-HE inventory. They are not comprehensive legal findings that these organizations cannot provide any higher education. No organization is excluded from the raw input, and no potential historical overlap (including Westerdals/Kristiania) is deduplicated without further evidence.
