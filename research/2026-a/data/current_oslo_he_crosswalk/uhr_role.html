About UHR

Universities Norway (UHR) is an interest organisation for accredited universities and university colleges in Norway. 

Published 06.04.2018 10.57

Universities Norway (UHR) is a cooperative body for 35 accredited universities and university colleges. The organisation works to promote quality, coordination, and the division of responsibilities within the higher education sector, both nationally and internationally.

Universities Norway (UHR) is an interest organisation for accredited universities and university colleges in Norway. The organisation has 35 member institutions.

UHR’s highest authority is the Council of Representatives, which elects the Board. The Board serves as the organisation’s executive body.

Universities Norway has 14 strategic units: eight disciplinary strategic units and six national strategic units. The strategic units serve as national coordination arenas for the member institutions and provide advice to UHR’s Board.

Our extensive networks enable the organisation to draw on specialised academic and administrative expertise from across the entire sector.
