# Tokyo PT school-stage category check

The 2018 Tokyo Metropolitan Area Person Trip Survey records age, occupation, trip purpose, endpoints and trip times. Its published category definitions include age bands 5–9, 10–14 and 15–19; occupation includes a single “nursery/pupil/student” category. The consulted published age/occupation/purpose categories do not isolate an upper-secondary or exact ages 14–18 group. An age-plus-student cross-tab alone cannot identify that target; this is not an exhaustive claim about every field in restricted individual records.

The survey covers residents of Tokyo (excluding islands) and neighboring prefectures. Its zone system includes Tokyo’s wards as resident-origin geography, so a custom tabulation may be able to aggregate the 23 wards. The official handbook describes expanded estimates using regional resident-population controls, but this audit did not create an account or run a tabulation. No duration cell, student-stage denominator or Tokyo-23-ward estimate was obtained. Do not admit a travel value to the model from this survey without a matching tabulation and its weights, year, geography and precision.

Official sources: [survey scope and questionnaire items](https://www.tokyo-pt.jp/person/04_01), [zone and category definitions](https://www.tokyo-pt.jp/data/01_01), [data aggregation system](https://www.tokyo-pt.jp/data/system_general_info), [official handbook](https://www.tokyo-pt.jp/static/hp/file/data/tebiki.pdf). The handbook defines the generic student occupation category and five-year age bands; detailed category combinations may have insufficient statistical precision.

A single raw HTTP retrieval attempt was made for each of the three specified official URLs. TLS certificate verification failed before HTTP response, so no status, body, byte count beyond zero, or content hash is available. No cookies were sent and no URL was retried. See `retrievals.json` for attempt/final UTC timestamps and errors. Category details were consulted through available official-source text extraction. They are provisional route notes rather than byte-reproducible claims from a locally obtained handbook. No account or data query was made.
