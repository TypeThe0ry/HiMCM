# Zurich city school-location teacher FTE bound

**Admissions: 0.** The official sources reviewed here do not identify a compatible numerical pair consisting of a verified city-school upper-secondary teaching-FTE lower bound and a pupil upper bound covering the same city-school population. No pupil–teacher ratio or utility interval is calculated or admitted.

The [cantonal school-personnel analysis](https://www.zh.ch/de/bildung/bildungssystem/zahlen-fakten/entwicklung-des-schulpersonals-an-mittelschulen-und-berufsfachschulen.html) distinguishes instructional FTE from headcount, but its published scope is publicly financed Mittelschulen and vocational schools across the canton. Leadership and teaching roles are both surveyed; a city-only, upper-secondary teaching allocation was not established from that page. A canton-wide FTE total is not a lower bound on city-only FTE. The [SSP survey description](https://www.zh.ch/de/bildung/bildungssystem/studien-in-der-bildung/lehrpersonen-und-schulpersonal.html) describes a census of teaching and leadership activities at schools located in the canton, including public and private suppliers; this establishes data collection scope, not a published city-stage numerical subset.

The [Mittelschulen pupil analysis](https://www.zh.ch/de/bildung/bildungssystem/zahlen-fakten/lernende-an-mittelschulen.html) distinguishes school types and mentions private financing; the [cantonal pocket-statistics description](https://www.zh.ch/de/bildung/bildungssystem/zahlen-fakten/taschenstatistik-die-schulen-im-kanton-zuerich.html) identifies school location as its geography. These descriptions do not by themselves establish a numerical all-provider upper bound for the precise target city upper-secondary cohort. Gymnasial programmes include lower-secondary years, while Mittelschulen and vocational schools use different groupings; stage and provider coverage require explicit matching. Even if a suitable canton-wide pupil upper bound is available, a verified city-school teaching-FTE lower bound remains necessary.

A future compatible pair could conditionally imply `PTR <= pupil_upper / city_teaching_FTE_lower` and utility `>= 15 / (15 + PTR_upper)`, with upper utility 1, only after verifying the date, school-location nesting, ISCED stage, teacher duties, and pupil/provider coverage. This is a bound conditional on the definitions and counts, not complete identification.
