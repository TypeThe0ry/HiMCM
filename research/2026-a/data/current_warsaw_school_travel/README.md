# Warsaw school-travel evidence: bounded source check

**Decision:** No new admission. The Warsaw travel-time utility remains fully unknown, [0, 1]. The materials in this folder do not establish a public, reproducible distribution of actual one-way school journeys for Warsaw-resident upper-secondary students or ages 14–18.

- `wbr2025_announcement.html` is the Warsaw road authority's announcement of the 2024 metropolitan and 2025 Warsaw household survey. Under “Ankieterzy zapytają między innymi o,” it lists trip purposes, transport modes, and trip start and end times. The announcement does not demonstrate a publicly available student-level duration distribution, school stage, age selection, or weights sufficient for the target. A survey question is not itself an observed distribution.
- `student_flows_2024_25.html` is the Warsaw Statistical Office landing page for its 2024/25 Mazowieckie primary and upper-secondary student commuting publication. It identifies commuting flows, not an actual one-way travel-time distribution for Warsaw residents. The landing page alone does not satisfy the target.
- `wbr2015_stage_iii.pdf.receipt.json` records the attempted official 2015 stage III report retrieval: HTTP 202, zero bytes. No PDF was obtained, and the link was not retried. An online indexed extraction of that report identifies printed page 29 as describing a combined database of 17,000 Warsaw-resident and 1,784 surrounding-agglomeration interviews, weighted by geography, sex, age and household size. This describes an existing weighted survey database, not proof of public microdata availability or target-field accessibility. The database mentioned on printed page 67 concerns traffic counts and should not be represented as household microdata. These report locators remain provisional until the actual PDF can be inspected.

Each `.receipt.json` records request URL, final URL, UTC retrieval time, HTTP status, selected response headers, response-body byte count and SHA-256. The saved HTML body is the content decoded by the HTTP client; `Content-Length`, where present, may describe the encoded transfer. Cookies and cookie headers were not recorded. Source dates, geographic scope and eligibility are not changed to fit the model. No model or manuscript files were changed as part of this check.
