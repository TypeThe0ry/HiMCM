## ADE SDER manual, October2021, 12pages

Source: https://www.azed.gov/sites/default/files/2021/10/SDER%20Manual%20Revised%2010.7.21.pdf

Successful reader response: `turn1118view0`, PDF page1 lines7–25, page3 lines84–101, pages4–5 lines135–149.

- Grade-level assignment on PDF page3 explicitly separates elementary(K–8), secondary(9–12), and combination. Exact short excerpt: “E = Elementary (K-8) / S = Secondary (9-12) / C = Combination”.
- Page1 requires the appropriate FTE allocation for employees assigned to two schools, but permits itinerant teachers to be recorded at one school or split between two. School attribution therefore need not identify all actual sites served.
- Page3 defines FTE relative to the district-approved full school day or class load and gives a part-time load example. It is employment FTE, not an observed measure of instructional effectiveness.
- Pages4–5 distinguish collected purchased-service personnel from teachers included in the Teacher Experience Index calculation; the two pools are not interchangeable. Intergovernmental-agreement employees are excluded from SDER reporting under the manual.

The current official manual directory also linked this manual during the successful review (`turn1122view0`, lines120–124): https://www.azed.gov/finance/school-finance-external-guidelines . The document itself remains dated October2021; its current listing does not independently establish every2024–25 implementation detail.
