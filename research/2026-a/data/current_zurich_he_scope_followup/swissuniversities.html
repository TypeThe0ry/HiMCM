Accredited Swiss Higher Education Institutions in accordance with HEdA*

This list of accredited Swiss higher education institutions is maintained by swissuniversities for information purposes only.

Under the Higher Education Act (HEdA), responsibility for accreditation lies with the Swiss Accreditation Council, which acts as the decision-making body on accreditation matters. Enquiries regarding accreditation should therefore be addressed directly to the Swiss Accreditation Council (info@akkreditierungsrat.ch).
