# Zurich teaching FTE: official custom-statistics route

**Admission: 0.** The Zurich cantonal Bildungsplanung offers tailored education statistics, including learners and school personnel, and gives a school-by-municipality count as an example. Its page states: “Sie erhalten massgeschneiderte Daten und Informationen zu Lernenden, Schulpersonal und Abschlüssen.” This identifies a source for requesting a matched aggregate, not a released FTE or a pupil–teacher ratio. The page does not establish that any particular breakdown will be supplied.

Source: [Anfragen an die Bildungsplanung](https://www.zh.ch/de/bildungsdirektion/generalsekretariat-der-bildungsdirektion/bildungsplanung/anfragen-an-die-bildungsplanung.html); the unmodified HTTP response and its acquisition metadata and SHA-256 are alongside this note. The official contact is bista@bi.zh.ch. No request was sent.

A useful inquiry would specify the city of Zurich as school location; public and private providers; 2024/25 and the respective reference dates; teaching FTE separated from management; and matched learner counts. It should ask for the components by general upper-secondary, vocational, bridging and special/remote programmes, plus missing reporting, denominator definitions and rounding, so the response can be checked against the actual target population. A requested breakdown is not evidence of availability or eligibility.

The existing V120 city workbook's T_3 upper-secondary subtotal of 8,581 (2024/25) is for Mittelschulen, not every city upper-secondary school. It cannot establish a complete target population by itself. No user data, weights, intervals, model outputs, or manuscript were changed.
