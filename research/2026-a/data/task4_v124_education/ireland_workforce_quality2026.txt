2. Introduction

This Summary Quality Report provides detailed background information on the Teacher Workforce Data
tables published by the Department of Education & Youth on the Central Statistics Office’s (CSO) PxStat
Open Data platform. These tables are based on data from the DEY and ETB’s payroll systems, and the
Teaching Council registration datasets. They provide data on teacher workforce counts in Irish schools
funded by the state. The data is sourced by the Department of Education and Youth. This data is also used
along with school data from the Primary Online Database (POD) and the Post Primary Online Database
(PPOD) datasets in the “Workforce Data Bulletin” which is published by DEY annually.

5. Statistical Presentation
5.1. Data Description
The teacher workforce data tables consist of a total count of whole-time equivalent teachers broken down
by contract type and school sector. They are presented on PX Stat as full time equivalent of full time or
parttime teacher counts by school sector. They are further broken down by school type within each sector.
The DEY annual publication tables also include total counts grouped by county or ETB level.


5.2. Classification System
The three main classifications used are School Sector, School Type, Full Time Equivalent (FTE).

School sector is Primary or Post Primary.

School Type is the type of Primary or Post Primary school grouping for example: “Ordinary with special
classes” primary school or “Vocational” post primary school.

The FTE is a calculation of the total full time equivalent teachers. If a teacher only works half a school
week, they are counted as 0.5 FTE, if they work full time they are counted as 1 FTE. FTEs are first
calculated at school level, distinguished by their PPSN and the school’s unique Roll Number. They are not
published at this level. Calculations are aggregated to county level for DEY annual publication and “School
Type” level for PX Stat publications.


5.3. Sector Coverage
Data covers all primary and post primary teachers who are paid by the Department of Education and
Youth or ETBs and who are contracted in Primary (including Special Schools) or Post Primary schools
funded by the state. Teachers who work in private schools are counted where their salary is paid by the
Department of Education and Youth.

13.     Accuracy and reliability
The main data source for this publication are administrative datasets. These datasets record individuals
who have been paid or have paid a salary or registration fee and therefore the data is extremely accurate
and reliable. Data is taken from these sources at multiple times throughout the year to allow for checking
and verification. The business teams who produce the datasets carry out both technical and operational
checks prior to providing the data to the statistics team, who in turn carry out their own verification.


14.     Timeliness and punctuality

17.3.   Data Validation

All datasets are checked by appropriate technical teams to ensure technical extraction was carried out
successfully, for example data integrity and completeness and data quality.

The operational teams then check actual contents of files to ensure correctness, for example comparisons
to previous extractions and spot checks on individual entries. When they are confident that the files are
accurate, they transfer to the statistics team.

The statistics team carry out their own checks on the files to ensure transfer was successfully completed
with no errors. They also pseudonymise the data to ensure no individual can be identified. The files are
stored in folders with restricted access.

The aggregate data is developed using R. The aggregate data is validated by the statistics team.

Our Quality statement can be found here:
https://www.gov.ie/en/department-of-education/collections/quality-reports-and-metadata/



17.4.   Data Compilation
Operations are performed on data to derive new information according to a given set of rules.

Each entry in the payroll files has an associated unique PPSN and either a school Roll Number or in the
case of ETB schools a Cost Centre number. Roll numbers and Cost Centre numbers are unique to each
school. The full time equivalent (FTE) value per PPSN is calculated. Then using the roll number/cost
centre and the FTE value associated with each PPSN, total full time estimates per school are calculated.

Teaching Council data is used to verify teacher types in ETB schools to distinguish post primary teachers
from other teaching staff.

POD and PPOD data is used to add additional school data to the calculations e.g.: school type, DEIS, Irish
Medium School etc. POD/PPOD data is also used to check the open/closed status of the school. Longitude
and latitude values are gathered using the school address data from these databases to allow for
geographical mapping of results.
Aggregates are calculated by various groupings, contract types, school types, FTE verses allocated posts.
All aggregates are dependent on the original FTE per PPSN calculation.

Results are compared to previous time periods and time series are recorded for this reason.
