# Zurich school-trip route: no matching published distribution located

**Admission: 0.** A bounded official-source search did not find a published 2021 or 2025 Zurich municipality school-trip one-way duration distribution, or observation-level provenance and matching duration bins for the retained 2024 Eurostat 39.9-minute greater-city work-commute estimate. The user CSV and the model are unchanged. This is a search finding, not proof such data do not exist.

The city’s [2021 mobility article](https://www.stadt-zuerich.ch/artikel/de/statistik-und-daten/modalsplit-bilanz-in-zeiten-der-pandemie.html) reports 5,493 sampled paths by persons interviewed across Switzerland with a start and/or end in the city, across purposes; it is neither a distribution of school-trip durations nor a Zurich-resident student sample. The [BFS national 2021 report](https://www.swissstats.bfs.admin.ch/data/webviewer/appId/ch.admin.bfs.swissstat/article/issue23118402100-06/package) defines education trips broadly to include schools, universities and courses and assigns return-home trips to a purpose; a distribution by education purpose alone therefore does not establish a one-way home-to-school distribution. Its national result is not municipal. The [2005 municipal analysis](https://www.stadt-zuerich.ch/content/dam/web/de/politik-verwaltung/statistik-und-daten/publikationen-dienstleistungen/publikationen/analysen/A_014_2005.pdf) defines its one-way door-to-door travel time for **work** and uses 1990–2000 observations; it is not a current city school-trip series. The [2025 microcensus leaflet](https://dam-api.bfs.admin.ch/hub/api/dam/assets/33417838/master) announced first results for mid-2027; this is a planned release, not proof of current availability or future municipal disaggregation. The [ARE page](https://www.are.admin.ch/de/mzmv) describes a data-protection agreement for research-institution access to anonymised survey data; access for this project has not been established.

**Gate for an independent school-trip extension:** document population and age/school level, residence versus school location and municipal boundary, survey year and reference days, direction (home to educational institution), door-to-door unit including waits/transfers, per-person versus per-trip denominator and survey weights, bin endpoints, missing data and uncertainty. Establish whether observed estimates support the desired inference before using them; an education-purpose average, distance bins or round-trip mix is insufficient. Any valid 2021 or later school scenario is separate from the 2024 greater-city work-commute baseline, not a replacement or a deterministic constraint on its population.

**Gate for tightening the existing 39.9 input:** establish the original 2024 CH002C observation’s production, exact greater-city boundary, survey window, inclusion/exclusion criteria, weighting and one-way travel-time definition, then obtain bins or support for that same weighted population and period. A confidence interval on a mean is not a bound on individual trip durations. Eurostat's estimated flag remains material; the matching published record does not establish the CSV's full provenance chain.
