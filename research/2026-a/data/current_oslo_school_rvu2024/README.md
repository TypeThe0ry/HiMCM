# 2024 travel survey: Oslo-region school trips

Figure 28, printed p. 51 (PDF page 52), reports the distribution of trip duration to/from school in the Oslo-området in 2024. The chart was visually inspected in `figure28_page51.png`; `report.txt` is the layout-preserving PDF extraction. The six displayed percentages are:

| Minutes | Share |
| --- | ---: |
| 1–4 | 2% |
| 5–9 | 12% |
| 10–19 | 33% |
| 20–29 | 22% |
| 30–59 | 28% |
| 60 or more | 3% |

The chart labels the regional series n = 610. These are displayed whole percentages, not recovered exact shares or counts. They sum to 100% as displayed; that does not establish the underlying unrounded values or the rounding rule. The final bin has no upper endpoint. No mean or distribution within bins can be determined from this chart alone.

## Scope and method

The report's survey population is Norwegian residents aged 13 and older (p. 17). The chart is for trips to/from school, not a high-school-only population; the report also refers to pupils/students and school/study locations (p. 20). Daily trips are classified by destination purpose, and trips ending at home by the preceding trip's purpose (p. 16); trips are the units shown rather than necessarily distinct students. The figure does not establish a separate age or school-level breakdown. The Oslo-området contains 20 municipalities (p. 2): Oslo, Bærum, Asker, Lillestrøm, Ullensaker, Nesodden, Frogn, Vestby, Ås, Enebakk, Lørenskog, Rælingen, Aurskog-Høland, Nes, Gjerdrum, Nittedal, Nannestad, Eidsvoll, Hurdal, and Nordre Follo. It is not Oslo municipality alone.

The chapter 3–9 results use the national weight, constructed with sex, age, geography, and travel day; the report states that subregional analyses generally require a tailored regional weight (pp. 23–24). The reported 2024 response rate is 9% (p. 23). The report cautions that national weights have high values and national results require care; the size of any corresponding error in this figure is not provided. The Oslo municipality appears separately for several other indicators in chapter 9, but no municipality-only school-duration or secondary-age breakdown was identified in this report. It refers to a separate 2024 documentation note supplied with the dataset (p. 17) and a separate weighting documentation note (p. 24); availability and contents were not verified.

This is independent contextual evidence and is not a replacement for the existing Oslo 31-minute / 2009 input, nor an admission into primary V203. Differences in year, geography, population, and trip definitions prevent treating the figures as matched.
