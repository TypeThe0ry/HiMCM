Congratulations and welcome to the Phoenix Union High School District. Everyone is becoming
increasingly aware of the traffic congestion and air pollution problems facing the Valley of the Sun.
As members of the community, Phoenix Union supports efforts to reduce air pollution and improve
our quality of life. We encourage you whenever possible, to share a ride to work by carpooling or
vanpooling, taking the bus or light rail, riding your bike, or if you live close, walking to work.
Our district, like hundreds of other companies in the Valley participates in the Maricopa County Trip
Reduction Program. The goal of the program is to reduce drive alone travel to work. In our effort to
comply with the program and make commuting less stressful and more convenient for you, we offer
several commute related incentives and subsidies. These benefits include:
