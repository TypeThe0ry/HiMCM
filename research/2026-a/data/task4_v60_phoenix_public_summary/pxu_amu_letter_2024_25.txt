To: Phoenix Union Employees
Re: Maricopa County Clean Air
Greetings,
I am sure you are all aware of the Maricopa County Trip Reduction initiative. This is the
program enforced by the County to reduce carbon emissions into our air by reducing single trips to
and from work and encouraging workers to use mass transit and other alternate modes of
transportation. Phoenix Union provides an Alternate Mode User (AMU) incentive program to
support this initiative. Every time you use an alternate method of transportation to commute to work,
i.e. bus, carpool, van pool, light rail, bike, walk, etc., visit your campus trip reduction coordinator
and sign the log. Here is a list of the campus vehicle trip reduction coordinators:
