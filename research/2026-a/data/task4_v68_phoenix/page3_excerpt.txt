Staff (562.2 FTEs included in the FY2026 approved appropriated funds budget)

        ASDB has staff at its two school campuses and at its three regional cooperatives that are located in various parts
        of the State. As of October 1, 2025, there were 585.07 full-time equivalents (FTEs) and 659 people employed to
        implement its agency responsibilities. The new payroll system calculates FTE differently than the old payroll
        system. This change has increased our calculated FTE by more than 100 FTE overall.

        There are currently 66 vacancies actively being recruited, which include 16 teachers; 11 instructional assistants;
        2 interpreters; 7 drivers; and 30 various other positions. Positions are still being justified and reviewed prior to
        posting.
