Financial Summary

ASDB continues to maintain financial and operational transparency, provide timely financial updates to
stakeholders and communicate progress on new initiatives while investing in strategic priorities. Efforts to
implement operational efficiencies and ongoing cost-savings initiatives continue.

The financial information as presented has been extracted from the State’s official accounting system
Arizona Financial Information System (AZ360 formerly AFIS).

BFY2026 Budget Overview

Budgetary control is maintained through Legislative appropriation and the Executive branch allotment
process. The Governor is required to submit an annual budget to the Legislature. The budget is legally
required to be adopted through the passage of appropriation bills by the Legislature and approved by the
Governor. The appropriated funds are controlled by the Executive branch through an allotment process.
This process generally allocates the appropriation into quarterly allotments by legal appropriation level.
The State also maintains an encumbrance accounting system to further enhance budgetary control.
Encumbered amounts generally lapse as of the end of the fiscal year, with the exception of capital outlay
and other continuing appropriations that continue from year to year.

Key messages about the accompanying budgetary statements for the months ended September 30, 2025 and
2024 are provided on the succeeding pages noting that September represents three months (25%) of the
fiscal year. Note: Budget fiscal year (BFY) data relates to transactions for the period of which the revenue
was earned, or expenditure was incurred. Fiscal year (FY) data includes accrued transactions (i.e., revenue
was earned in a prior budget year but not yet received until the next year or expense was incurred in a
prior budget year but not yet paid until the next year).

    •   ASDB’s FY2026 approved appropriations budget totaled $68.6 million. Of that total, $58.9 million
        or 86% are monies allocated by legislation to ASDB consisting of the General Fund of $25.6
        million, the Cooperative Services Fund of $19.1 million and the Schools for the Deaf and the Blind
        Fund of $14.2 million. Other sources are included in the budget totaling $9.7 million or 14%
        comprised of other non-appropriated funds (revenue generating) of $5.6 million and federal funds
        of $4.1 million.
    •   Based on recent projections of revenues, the internal budget was revised to $68.4 million less $5.6
        million in voucher reimbursements to the districts.

Institutional voucher reimbursements are provided to school districts with students receiving services from the
Itinerant Services Program and are funded from the institutional voucher. We have changed how the
reimbursements are processed and are tracking them separately. Previously the reimbursements were recorded
as a reduction in expenses, we are now recording them as a reduction in revenue since we are refunding voucher
revenue to the districts. There are $518,400 in voucher reimbursements for prior years, $137,362 related to
BFY21 through BFY24 and $381,038 related to BFY25. In response to the sunset audit, we gave the districts
that did not invoice for voucher reimbursement in BFY21 to BFY24 another opportunity to submit invoices.
