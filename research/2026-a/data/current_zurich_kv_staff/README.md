# Zurich: a primary employer's approximate occupied teacher positions

A new primary annual report separates approximate teacher full-time positions from teacher headcount. The Wirtschaftsschule KV Zürich panel of the Kaufmännischer Verband Zürich2024annual report displays about134teacher full-time positions,185teacher persons and42persons in full-time mode; administration is about32positions/40persons/16full-time-mode persons. The report describes average occupied positions, with166total positions and about225people. These are employer-reported measures, retained with their approximation and unit distinctions.

The source is [the association's2024annual report](https://www.kfmv-zuerich.ch/portals/0/downloads/generalversammlung/2025/kfmv_zuerich_jahresbericht_2024.pdf?1763251200189=), retrieved successfully as an original15,401,309-byte PDF. Physical PDFpage10contains a spread with printed pages18and19. The white left panel is Wirtschaftsschule KV Zürich; the blue right panel is the separately reported KV Business School Zürich. Source values were visually checked on the rendered spread and extracted by bounding-box selection of its left half. Full-spread text extraction interleaves the two institutions, so it is not used to attribute their numbers.

A school/provider report is a valid primary source; private status alone does not disqualify it. The teaching-employment measure is also not rejected for including ordinary preparation and other normal contract duties: a contact-hours conversion is not required for a compatible contract FTE. Admission instead remains unresolved because the source gives no quantified error bound around its approximation, the exact average/reference-period convention is not supplied here, and allocation of all2024reported employment to the target upper-secondary school/site population is unverified. The two requested current organisation/contact pages returned403and are retained only as failed responses. External index/browser leads about current sites do not prove the2024site inventory.

The42full-time-mode people are retained as a separate headcount/status statement, without automatically converting them into42annual teaching FTE. Likewise,185teacher people are not134precise FTE,166includes administration, and no school-entry/student-cohort figure is divided by them. A documented compatible teacher subset could support an envelope with proved containment and a matched pupil upper bound; this package obtains neither that contract nor a city ratio. The target Zurich PTR utility remains[0,1], with zero primary admissions and zero actual width reduction.

All raw bodies remain byte-for-byte as received.
