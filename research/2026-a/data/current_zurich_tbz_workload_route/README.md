# Zurich TBZ teaching-workload route

**Decision: admit 0 teaching FTE.** Technische Berufsschule Zürich's 2024 report does not supply a verified actual teaching full-time-equivalent total. Its 173 teachers include department leadership and are listed as people, with no employment percentages or allocation of teaching duties. The report also includes continuing education, so the staff figure cannot be assigned wholly to the upper-secondary vocational programmes. The date of the count and its match to 2024/25 are not stated in the figures section. No headcount-to-FTE conversion is justified.

The school's 2024 report, pp. 33–34 (PDF pages 33–34), lists 3,559 basic vocational education learners (3,132 without and 427 with BM), 240 continuing-education students, and 173 teachers including department leadership. These provide context but do not establish a compatible disjoint subset of 2024/25 city pupils with a measured teaching-FTE numerator. Its separate figures for administration, apprentices and school commission do not resolve teaching workload. The PDF is a 2024 report; do not assume its figures represent the 2024/25 school year. The 2024 report is the nearest report to the target shown on the school publications page; that page also lists a 2025/26 report, and no 2024/25 report is identified there.
