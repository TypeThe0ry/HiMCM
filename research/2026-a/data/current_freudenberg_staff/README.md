# Freudenberg staff-source scope check

**Decision: no admissible teaching-FTE lower bound identified.** The official Gymnasium Freudenberg download page lists annual reports through 2023/24 and a 2022 external evaluation. The two most recent linked annual reports and the evaluation were inspected as searchable PDF text on 2026-11-10. A search for German terms covering FTE, posts, workload and teaching hours found no relevant FTE figure in their extracted text. Text extraction does not exclude information present solely in images.

The 2023/24 annual report (PDF page 47, printed page 91) names temporary instructors, substitutes and teachers from other schools, without their workloads. The 2022 evaluation (PDF page 8) states that 120 teachers and 874 students were invited to a survey; these are invitation counts, not FTE or a citywide enrollment denominator. Its survey spans classes 1–6; the school has lower- as well as upper-gymnasium students. No actual teaching workload allocated to the upper-secondary stage was identified. Headcounts, class counts, allocations and full-school workloads cannot establish the requested stage-specific teaching FTE. No pupil-to-teacher ratio is calculated, and V203 inputs remain unchanged.

Source URLs, retrieval details and byte hashes are in `sources.json`. The exact re-fetched index HTML and PDF response bodies are preserved in `raw/`, their extracted text in `text/`, and the actual UTC times, HTTP status, response headers, byte sizes and SHA-256 hashes in `retrieval_receipt.json`. The three re-fetched PDFs match the initially inspected copies byte-for-byte; the initial request timestamps and headers were not retained. The two specifically cited pages were also visually inspected. PDF text was inspected using `pdftotext -layout`, and targeted searches covered `vollzeit`, `stellenproz`, `stellenetat`, `stellenplan`, `pensen`, `pensum`, `lektionenzahl`, `anzahl lektionen` and `beschäftigungsgrad`. A compatible citywide all-provider upper-secondary pupil upper bound and a matching-period, upper-stage actual teaching-FTE lower bound would both be required for a valid ratio upper bound.
