# Helsinki staffing: personnel reporting is not a matched teacher denominator

The original 27-page municipal personnel appendix is readable and preserved. Its introductory page explicitly reports at sector, office/agency and municipal-enterprise level. It does not establish actual teaching FTE for upper-secondary schools located in Helsinki, so the missing school-resource utility stays `[0,1]`.

Source: City of Helsinki, [Personnel report 2024 statistical appendix](https://www.hel.fi/static/kanslia/Julkaisut/2025/helsingin_kaupungin_henkilostoraportti_2024_tilastoliite.pdf). PDF page 3 identifies the reporting units and states a 31 December 2024 snapshot except for accumulated measures. It also cautions that changes in definitions affect comparisons before and after 2021. An education-sector staffing total includes more roles and stages than the required teaching denominator; neither employer identity nor the presence of a full-/part-time column establishes school location or stage allocation.

The PDF body, byte count and hash are verified.
