## Supported comparisons

- **Local meeting places:** printed p. 27 (PDF p. 29), visually checked. Among grade 8 respondents, 66% rated the local provision of places to meet other young people in their free time very or fairly good; among Vg3 respondents, 41%. The question is: “Tenk på områdene rundt der du bor. Hvordan opplever du at tilbudet til ungdom er når det gjelder … Lokaler for å treffe andre unge på fritida”. The graph explicitly combines “svært bra” and “nokså bra”. This is a perceived provision measure.
- **Sports club participation:** printed p. 33 (PDF p. 35), visually checked. 70% of grade 8 respondents and 32% of Vg3 respondents reported participating in sports club activities at least once in the previous month. The question is: “Hvor mange ganger har du vært med på aktiviteter, møter eller øvelser i følgende organisasjoner, klubber eller lag den siste måneden?”; the relevant category is “Idrettslag”. This is reported participation, not access or unmet demand.

Survey context: January–March 2023, 83 Oslo lower- and upper-secondary schools, voluntary school-time questionnaire. Report p. 1 gives 14,667 lower-secondary and 10,595 upper-secondary participants, with 78% and 60% response respectively; overall 25,262 out of 36,455 enrolled, 69% (p. 80). These counts are survey-wide, not item- or grade-specific denominators; the selected charts do not report those denominators. Participants could skip items; some low-response questionnaires were excluded, and the report describes weighting by grade and district to match 2018 composition (pp. 79–80). The comparisons are cross-sectional, not longitudinal age effects, and school grades do not establish exact ages 14 and 18. They do not quantify model weights, individual preferences, causality, or fairness in facility access. They are contextual evidence for the existing 2025-baseline analysis, not inputs to the primary scoring model.
