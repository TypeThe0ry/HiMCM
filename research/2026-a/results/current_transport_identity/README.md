# Transport stop-unit review

A consistent provider-defined inventory standard supports 827 Oslo groups as a controlled candidate: 829 NSR parent-collapsed roots become 827 after the two pairs of exact normalized names are combined. This uses the same general evidence standard as Zurich's published 446-stop aggregate, which is accepted without an independent 2025 stop-by-stop physical audit. The candidate remains conditional on its source-defined unit and eligibility; V202 retains the existing 89-station subset until admission and independent model review.

NSR's parent grouping distinguishes mode-specific child records from the group. The two equal-name pairs are merged conservatively, without claiming that they are physically identical. Two other differently named cross-mode pairs have representative points within 40 m; their names and distances are recorded in `method_review.json`. A short distance is a reason to inspect source identity, not proof of duplication. The exported register also needs a targeted review of non-passenger, planned and retired entries. Such a review should use the same standard applied to other retained sources, rather than demand perfect proof of real-time service at every station.

The 827 figure is an administrative inventory candidate, not a verified universal physical-stop definition, a measure of teen access or a count to add to the existing 89. The 517 distance/name clusters remain an unvalidated sensitivity scenario. Other modes and uncounted facilities retain an upper bound of 1. Evidence, comparator scopes, hashes and pre-admission gates are in `method_review.json`.
