# Prague PID bus nodes: scheduled service on 10 November 2026

All **942 of the 942 previously defined candidate PID nodes** have at least one matching published bus stop-time on 2026-11-10 with ordinary pickup/drop-off (GTFS value 0 or blank) or pickup/drop-off after coordination with the driver (value 3). Fifty nodes have an ordinary 0/blank witness; 892 additional nodes have a value-3 witness. A total of 926 nodes have a value-3 witness, overlapping the 50. No candidate requires a value-2-only (telephone coordination) witness. These are distinct native nodes, not distinct physical facilities. The earlier 942 are a restricted 100–250 route subset, not all bus facilities; metro overlap must be deduplicated.

PID's [request-stop policy](https://pid.cz/vsechny-zastavky-autobusu-a-trolejbusu-v-ramci-pid-budou-na-znameni/) says all PID bus and trolleybus stops adopted the request regime on 29 June 2024; it says a visible waiting passenger suffices for boarding and a passenger should press a stop button before alighting. The locally archived source is `../../data/current_prague_gtfs_service/pid_request_stop.html`. This policy supports interpreting driver-coordination stops as public conditional supply; it does not remove the coordination requirement or establish accessibility for every passenger. The [GTFS Schedule Reference](https://gtfs.org/documentation/schedule/reference/#stop_timestxt) defines 0/blank as regularly scheduled, 1 as unavailable, 2 as telephone arrangement and 3 as driver coordination. One public direction is sufficient: an alighting-only terminus may qualify. Type 2 is retained separately, never treated as 0 or 3; 1/1 is excluded.

The 2026-11-10 ROPID feed covers 2026-11-10 through 2026-11-23. A witness requires the exact archived list node and group name plus passenger-visible 100–250 route matched to the later JSON `type=bus` line and its exact `node/post` pole. A GTFS physical stop must share both `asw_node_id` and `asw_stop_id`, have `location_type=0` (or blank), and `zone_region_type=1`; the GTFS route must share that passenger-visible name, have `route_type=3`, `is_night=0` and `is_substitute_transport=0`. Candidate filters also exclude flagged archived timetable links and explicit JSON `isNight=true`; all selected JSON `isNight` values are absent, not false. GTFS calendar weekday/range and date additions/removals are applied to every trip. A matched stop-time must have a 0/blank or 3 in either pickup or drop-off. No name/proximity-only pole join and no internal line-ID join is used. Multiple virtual GTFS fare records sharing an ASW pole do not become extra nodes.

`public_witness.csv` has one witness per candidate node, preferring ordinary and otherwise driver coordination; `witness.csv` contains the 50 ordinary witnesses, and `conditional_witness.csv` all 926 with a driver-coordination witness. `phone_witness.csv` has headers and no rows. Exact list URL/locator, JSON pole and line locators, GTFS stop, route, service, date exception, trip, sequence, times and boarding codes are recorded. `pair_diagnostics.csv` retains all 3,669 distinct qualifying node/post/visible-route combinations and active/inactive and mode/access diagnostics; `node_diagnostics.csv` retains all 942 candidates. Two pairs lack an eligible active public stop-time; another pair at each affected node supplies its witness. Occurrence counts are not service frequencies. Archived list entries failing the original eligibility filters remain in `../current_prague_bus_crosswalk/link_diagnostics.csv`.

This is scheduled supply on a single service day, not observed operation, an annual guarantee, daytime service, a proof of universal or student access, or a physical-site count. The archived list is from 2026-11-09, current pole JSON from 2026-11-10 (timestamp timezone unspecified), and the population used elsewhere is from 2025; these snapshots do not establish that the historical data were identical.
