# Helsinki upper-secondary resource data request — unsent

A usable source must identify pupils and actual teaching full-time equivalents for the same schools, programmes, providers and reference period. Municipal personnel headcounts, an education-sector person-year total and a citywide pupil count do not establish that pair.

## Proposed aggregate request

Please identify an existing published aggregate or provide aggregate data for general upper-secondary education in schools physically located in Helsinki (municipality code 091), preferably for a completed school year such as 2024/25 or 2025/26. State actual reference dates and publication/revision dates; retain an older available period rather than relabel it as 2025.

1. Pupil headcount and, if published, pupil FTE, with definitions for adults, part-time study, dual qualifications and preparatory programmes. Specify included school and programme categories.
2. Actual teaching FTE for those same programmes and school locations, with the official full-time basis and workload calculation. Distinguish teaching from leadership and non-teaching roles; explain treatment of leave, substitutes, trainees, hourly teachers and staff shared across schools or stages.
3. Provider coverage: all providers combined and public/private or municipal/other-provider subtotals where available. Identify excluded or unallocated schools, programmes, staff activities and withheld cells.
4. Reference alignment: distinguish a point-in-time staff snapshot, paid annual person-years and actual teaching workload. Provide the matching pupil reference or explain why dates cannot be aligned.
5. Geographic allocation: explain multi-site and cross-municipality institutions. An employer or provider address is not sufficient evidence of the location where the teaching is delivered.

Request only aggregate statistics and methodology, not personal records. A ready-made published ratio is useful if its numerator, denominator, scope and calculation can be traced.

## Acceptance after a response

Calculate a ratio only after numerator and denominator are compatible. A documented teaching-FTE subset could support a one-sided bound if its inclusion in the target denominator and a compatible complete pupil total are both established. A national estimate, municipal headcount, cross-stage workload or undefined person-year series cannot supply that containment by assumption.

Record the source URL or supplied file, statistical period, location and provider definition, units, extraction locator, revisions and any imputation or rounding. Missing or withheld fields stay unknown. Any candidate must pass independent source and numerical review before a controlled model admission; the original CSV remains unchanged.

Status: prepared supporting material, **not sent**. No response or new measurement is implied by this specification.
