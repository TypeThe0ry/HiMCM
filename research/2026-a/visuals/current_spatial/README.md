# Oslo and Zurich: modelled walking time across retained resident grids

The author-platform grids give a retained-resident-weighted share in cells with modelled mean walking time at most 30 minutes to 20 generic Learning POIs of **99.18% for Oslo** (681,203.843 / 686,862.091 resident-equivalents) and **99.97% for Zurich** (401,412.740 / 401,551.566). The threshold applies to each cell's modelled mean, not individual trips. Residents are not identified as students, Learning POIs are not verified high schools, and this illustration neither establishes student access nor changes V203 scores. The Oslo grid was captured on 10 November 2026 and Zurich on 7 November 2026; population and underlying input years and boundary comparability are unverified. The Oslo grid population differs from the model's supplied 727,797 denominator.

The 6.5-inch-wide figure uses actual EPSG:3857 projected cell geometry; north is up in the two-dimensional panels. All plotted positive-population cells have finite positive `c_f`, and the identical 0–150 minute color and extrusion scale applies to both cities. Height represents modelled walking time, not elevation or buildings. City extents differ. Empty areas include absent or zero-population cells and are not identified as water. Scale bars approximate ground distance at each city's latitude. Text is at least 9 pt at 6.5-inch insertion width.

Three Oslo NASA HLS S30/GIBS 2024-06-27, 06-22 and 06-17 WMS requests returned fully transparent images; Oslo therefore has geometry only. Zurich uses the previously archived 27 June 2024 WMS request; cloud is visible and no exact product-granule acquisition time has been verified. Background imagery supplies geographic context, not input to the walking-time calculations. The seven pre-existing Singapore and Zurich imagery responses and their manifests remain unchanged; no cloud or terrain is synthesized.

`build.py` verifies the Oslo download hash and Zurich original archive hash and computes the metrics offline, checking Oslo geometry falls in the broader Oslo region and its population sum matches the archived city-index rounded total. `render.py` builds the figure from those inputs; `fallback.py` verifies the unchanged historical MODIS fallback. Use `/data/projects/HiMCM/.venv-v120/bin/python research/2026-a/visuals/current_spatial/render.py`. Oslo source bytes and HTTP status/time/hash are in `research/2026-a/data/current_oslo_spatial/`; `oslo_imagery_manifest.json` records the three response bytes, URLs, dates, bounds, hashes and transparency. The prior `imagery_manifest.json` and `fallback_manifest.json` preserve the earlier responses. `metrics.csv` and `metrics.json` expose exact numerator, denominator, geographic bounds and source hashes. [NASA HLS S30](https://hls.gsfc.nasa.gov/products-description/s30/) and [GIBS WMS documentation](https://nasa-gibs.github.io/gibs-api-docs/access-basics/) describe the product and retrieval service.
