# City walking-time grids and satellite context

The retained resident-weighted share in grids with modelled mean walking time at most 30 minutes to 20 generic Learning POIs is **94.13% in Singapore** (5,484,655 / 5,826,786 retained residents) and **99.97% in Zurich** (401,412.740 / 401,551.566 resident-equivalents). Values come from the author platform's 2026-11-07 snapshot, not student-specific observations. These are modelled grid-level means; the threshold is applied to each grid mean, not to individual trips. The two city boundaries and source dates differ or are unspecified. This descriptive comparison is not a within-country pairing or a revision of the primary scores.

## Figure and interpretation

`city_walktime.pdf`, `.svg`, `.png` are a 6.5 × 6.4 inch figure with a 300 dpi PNG and vector text/maps (3D collections rasterized). Figure text is at least 9 pt **at that insertion size**. Shorten layout rather than scale down below that size. The upper maps depict positive-population cells using their measured geometry. Empty space includes zero-population or absent cells and must not be interpreted as water. The same EPSG:3857 bounds are used for each city's 2D and 3D plots; both extrusion heights and colors use the identical 0–150 minute scale, including Singapore's 147.22 minute maximum. Heights represent walking time, never terrain or building heights. City horizontal map extents differ; north is up in the 2D maps. Scale-bar distances approximate geodesic distance at the stated city latitude. The Zurich image is a 2024-06-27 WMS date request; a product-granule acquisition timestamp has not been independently confirmed. Clouds remain unaltered. Singapore has no backdrop because none of the bounded requests gave a usable land view.

Suggested caption: **Retained-resident exposure to modelled walking times.** In the archived author-platform grids, 94.13% of retained Singapore residents and 99.97% of retained Zurich residents live in cells whose modelled mean walking time to 20 generic Learning points of interest is at most 30 minutes. Heights encode that grid mean, with the same 0–150 minute scale in both cities; they do not represent terrain or buildings. Grids represent residents rather than students and do not measure high-school access. The NASA HLS S30 / GIBS Zurich browse image was requested for 27 June 2024 and contains cloud; no satellite image is shown for Singapore. Data snapshot accessed 7 November 2026; underlying city input dates are unstated.

## Sources and reproducibility

- Input ZIPs and original manifests: `research/2026-a/data/task4_access/README.md` and `manifest.json`. SHA-256 both match, recorded in `metrics.json`. All 3,657 Singapore and 703 Zurich positive-population grid times are finite and positive; unknown positive population is zero. Exact numerator, denominator, source hashes, bounds and counts appear in `metrics.json` and `metrics.csv`.
- `build.py` verifies hashes and computes raw geometry and resident-weighted shares while checking archived HLS responses. `fallback.py` checks the archived MODIS response. `render.py` builds the figure from the archived input and selected Zurich response. Run from repository root with `python research/2026-a/visuals/current_spatial/render.py`; dependencies: numpy, matplotlib and Pillow; requests is needed only for the original HTTP acquisition helper, not offline archive checks or rendering. Both scripts reuse and verify the existing archive offline; missing or mismatched responses cause failure. Fresh acquisition requires a separate new directory.
- NASA [HLS S30 description](https://hls.gsfc.nasa.gov/products-description/s30/) confirms a 30 m product; [GIBS access documentation](https://nasa-gibs.github.io/gibs-api-docs/access-basics/) describes the WMS service. [NASA media usage](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/) governs use of NASA imagery. Satellite imagery provides geographic context, not the accessibility calculation. `imagery_manifest.json` records each request URL, UTC retrieval, CRS, exact returned extent, pixel dimensions, SHA-256 and alpha coverage; `fallback_manifest.json` records the MODIS request. Output 1000 × 800 pixels is a rendering size, not additional sensor resolution.
- All six HLS responses are archived: Singapore's three dates returned fully transparent PNGs; Zurich 27 June had 99.821% nontransparent pixels, with visible city and substantial cloud, and its other two responses were transparent. The one 250 m MODIS Terra fallback for Singapore had 100% nontransparent pixels but the city is cloud-obscured; it was rejected from the figure. Alpha coverage measures nontransparency, not cloud-free coverage. `contact.png` is the unchanged candidate contact sheet.

## Checks and limitations

Manually inspected the response contact sheet, MODIS image and exported figure. Source geometry and imagery share the same projected bounding box, with north-up map rendering, and all populated cells fall within that bounding box. Zurich's lake and urban forms are visually consistent with the grid region where visible through clouds; no independent Singapore coastline overlay was used, so Singapore water placement was not verified and no water is depicted. An independent numerical recomputation by the coordinating agent agreed on retained populations and shares. Clouds obscure parts of the Zurich background, and actual product-granule timestamps and the completeness of retained city populations remain unverified. No confidence intervals are supplied by this source.
