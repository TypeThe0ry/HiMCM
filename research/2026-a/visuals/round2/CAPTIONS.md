# Figure captions and scope

Local research figures; not inserted into the live Overleaf manuscript.

## 01_framework

Model construction and revision. Panel 4 separates changes in the ranking model from information gain under fixed weights. Learning-facility accessibility is the spatial opportunity proxy.

Style reference: 2024 Team 14995, PDF p.4, Figure 1; 2024 Team 15504, PDF p.13

## 02_spatial_access

Learning-facility accessibility in three illustrative cities. Each populated source grid is coloured by model utility. Colours cover retained populated grids. Panel extents differ; local distance conversion and scale bars are approximate. Cases illustrate a stable leader (Copenhagen), upward revision (Toronto) and downward revision (Dubai).

Style reference: 2023 Team 13719, PDF p.15, Figure 5.6: aligned spatial panels; 2025 Team 16738, PDF p.9: shared geographical colour scale

## 03_access_mechanism

Accessibility distributions and the lower-tail mechanism for three illustrative cities. Distributions are weighted by retained all-age population. Panel (c) varies only the normative lower-tail weight; the curves show the resulting model utility response.

Style reference: 2023 Team 13719, PDF p.16, Figure 5.7: Times typography, thin boxed axes and navy/rose/amber lines; 2024 Team 15504, PDF p.18, Figure 8: grouped bars with direct values

## 04_city_ranking

Conditional ranking under the five-proxy model, with all 25 cities shown. Panel (b) separates the baseline, the addition of mean learning accessibility, and the lower-tail adjustment; vertical offsets separate the three markers for each city and carry no numerical meaning. The panel isolates changes in the evaluation specification.

Style reference: 2023 Team 13719, PDF p.22, Figure 6.3: ordered bars with meaningful colour groups; 2025 Team 16739, PDF p.9, Figure 6: horizontal ranking and direct values

## 05_evidence_uncertainty

Information gain under fixed weights: baseline proxies 80%, learning accessibility 10%, unobserved education 10%. Intervals span scores permitted by the unobserved indicator values. The three possible champions after observation are Copenhagen, Dubai and Melbourne. Additional proxy-error widths define the stress scenarios.

Style reference: 2023 Team 13719, PDF pp.16 and 22: restrained linework, grouped panels and consistent scales

## 06_indicator_matrix

Five proxy indicators for all 25 cities, ordered by the revised ranking. Columns retain every city; the scale is bounded from zero to one. The colour scale encodes benefit-oriented proxy scores.

Style reference: 2025 Team 16739, PDF p.8, Figure 1: annotated cells and explicit colour scale; adapted to a horizontal five-indicator layout
