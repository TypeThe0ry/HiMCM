The Consortium for Mathematics and its Applications (COMAP) is pleased to announce the 
selection of the two teams moving on to represent the USA in the 12th annual International 
Mathematical Modeling Challenge, (IM2C)®. These 18 teams were among the 273 teams from 
the United States that distinguished themselves in the 2025 High School Mathematical Contest 
in Modeling (HiMCM)® and Middle Mathematical Contest in Modeling (MidMCM) 
contests.

T
he International Mathematical Modeling Challenge, IM2C 
The purpose of the IM2C is to promote the teaching of mathematical modeling and applications 
at all educational levels for all students. The IM2C international organizers believe students and 
teachers need to experience the power of mathematics to help better understand, analyze and 
solve real world problems outside of mathematics itself – and to do so in realistic contexts. In the 
spirit of promoting educational change, COMAP launched The First annual IM2C in 2015.
