NEWS RELEASE  
2024 Middle Mathematical Contest in Modeling 
  
The Consortium for Mathematics and its Applications (COMAP) is pleased to announce the results of the 2024 Middle Mathematical Contest 
in Modeling (MidMCM). A total of 76 middle school/level teams, with up to 4 students each, representing 44 schools and 3 countries/regions, 
competed. All teams worked within the contest window of November 6-19, 2024. The MidMCM “The Modeling Musical Tour” required 
teams to analyze the musical performer’s past tours to develop a model to help plan their next tour.

COMAP commends the teams for their efforts. The judges were impressed with all the teams’ creativity and ingenuity in mathematical 
modeling, and in their ability to explain their strategies and problem-solving techniques in clear terms. Each participant is a true winner. 
A complete results report, listing all teams by designation, can be found at www.midmcm.org. For additional contest information, contact 
COMAP at midmcm@comap.org.
