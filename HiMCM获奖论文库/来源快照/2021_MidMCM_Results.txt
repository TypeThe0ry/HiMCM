2021 Middle Mathematical Contest in Modeling 
T
he Consortium for Mathematics and its Applications (COMAP) is pleased to announce the results of the 2021 Middle Mathematical Contest 
in Modeling (MidMCM). A total of 54 middle school/level teams, with up to 4 students each, representing 36 schools and 4 countries/regions, 
competed. All teams worked within the contest window of November 3-16, 2021. The MidMCM “Dog Park Problem” required teams to 
design a dog park as well as determine the materials and budget needed to build the park.

OMAP commends schools for their efforts. The judges were impressed with all the teams’ creativity and ingenuity in mathematical 
modeling, and in their ability to explain their strategies and problem-solving techniques in clear terms. Each participant is a true winner. A 
complete Results Report, listing all teams by designation, can be found at www.midmcm.com. For additional contest information, contact 
COMAP at midmcm@comap.com. 
MidMCM is made possible by COMAP, Inc.  Additional support is provided by  The Institute for Operations Research and the Management Sciences (INFORMS), 
The Mathematical Association of America (MAA), and The National Council of Teachers of Mathematics (NCTM). The Contest Director is Kathleen Snook , COMAP Inc.
