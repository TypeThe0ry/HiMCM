2025 High School Mathematical Contest in Modeling 
The Consortium for Mathematics and its Applications (COMAP) is pleased to announce the results of the 2025 High School Mathematical Contest in 
Modeling (HiMCM)®. A total of 1091 teams, with up to 4 students each, representing 498 schools and 24 countries/regions, competed. All teams worked 
within the contest window of November 5-18, 2025. Each team chose from two modeling problems offered and then constructed their solutions.
