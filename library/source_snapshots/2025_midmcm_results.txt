2025 Middle Mathematical Contest in Modeling 
The Consortium for Mathematics and its Applications (COMAP) is pleased to announce the results of the 2025 Middle Mathematical Contest 
in Modeling (MidMCM). A total of 108 middle school/level teams, with up to 4 students each, representing 53 schools and 6 
countries/regions, competed. All teams worked within the contest window of November 5-18, 2025. The MidMCM problem “Responsible 
and Interesting Zoos!” required teams to design a zoo on a 1-square-kilometer plot of land of their choosing.
