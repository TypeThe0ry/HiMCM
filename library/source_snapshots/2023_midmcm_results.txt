NEWS RELEASE  
2023 Middle Mathematical Contest in Modeling 
  
The Consortium for Mathematics and its Applications (COMAP) is pleased to announce the results of the 2023 Middle Mathematical Contest 
in Modeling (MidMCM). A total of 75 middle school/level teams, with up to 4 students each, representing 43 schools and 7 countries/regions, 
competed. All teams worked within the contest window of November 1-14, 2023. The MidMCM “Olympic Medal Ceremonies” required 
teams to determine how many medals and flags The International Olympic Committee (IOC) would need.

COMAP commends the teams for their efforts. The judges were impressed with all the teams’ creativity and ingenuity in mathematical 
modeling, and in their ability to explain their strategies and problem-solving techniques in clear terms. Each participant is a true winner. A 
complete results report, listing all teams by designation, can be found at www.midmcm.org. For additional contest information, contact 
COMAP at midmcm@comap.org.
