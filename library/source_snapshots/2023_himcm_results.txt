2023 High School Mathematical Contest in Modeling 
  
The Consortium for Mathematics and its Applications (COMAP) is pleased to announce the results of the 2023 High School Mathematical Contest in 
Modeling (HiMCM)®. A total of 967 teams, with up to 4 students each, representing 417 schools and 18 countries/regions, competed. All teams worked 
within the contest window of November 1-14, 2023. Each team chose from two modeling problems offered, and then constructed their solutions.
