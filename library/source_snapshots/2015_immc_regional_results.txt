NEWS RELEASE  
The Consortium for Mathematics and its Applications (COMAP) is pleased to announce the 
selection of the two teams moving on to represent the USA in the 1st annual International 
Mathematical Modeling Challenge, (IM2C). The teams were chosen from the 341 teams that 
represented the USA in the 2014 High School Mathematical Contest in Modeling (HiMCM)® 
contest.

The International Mathematical Modeling Challenge, IM2C 
The purpose of the IM2C is to promote the teaching of mathematical modeling and applications 
at all educational levels for all students. It is based on the firm belief that students and teachers 
need to experience the power of mathematics to help better understand, analyze and solve real 
world problems outside of mathematics itself – and to do so in realistic contexts. The Challenge 
is being launched in the spirit of promoting educational change.  
 
For more information about the IM2C visit: www.immchallenge.org.  
For more information about the HiMCM contest visit:  www.himcm.org.
