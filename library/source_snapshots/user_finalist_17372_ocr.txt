Summary Sheet

Research on a Location Selection Model Based on
the Combined Weighting Method

Abstract

To emphasize the ecological impact of mega-sport events, we established a comprehensive
evaluation and location selection model. This model takes into account different factors
driving positive or negative environmental impact, aiming to provide a scientific basis
regarding location selection choices for mega-sports events.

For Task 1, we selected six evaluation indicators regarding environmental sustainability, in-
cluding travel distances, air conditioning consumption, energy structures, transportation
availability, and weather conditions. Quantitative calculation expressions are established
for those indicators. Among the 19 cities that have previously hosted the Super Bowl,
we selected 3 new candidates: Denver CO, Nashville TN, and Seattle WA. The indicator
values for the 22 cities are then calculated. The detailed results are shown in Table 5.

For Task 2, we created a model using the combined weighting method. The Analytic
Hierarchy Process (AHP) and the entropy method are combined to calculate the final
weight matrix. Through further comparison with results from the VIKOR method with
the Kendall's coefficient of concordance, the results showed a strong and statistically
significant correlation, confirming the robustness and credibility of the method selected.

For Task 3, we calculated the final scores according to the weights determined by the
combined weighting method for the 22 candidate cities. The rankings are shown in Table 5
with Stanford CA leading all candidates with a score of 74.066. Denver CO leads the three
new candidate cities with a score of 43.435.

For Task 4, we identified extensions to existing indicators for different types of tournaments
and applied the model to select the most environmentally sustainable Formula I Grand Prix
location. Silverstone, United Kingdom appears as the most environmental sustainable
choice with a score of 79.981. We also identified strategies in Section 5.5.2, then applying
them to Los Angeles CA and Nashville TN. Both cities show significant increases in the
final scores (Figure 9), reflecting the effectiveness of the strategies.

For Task 5, we attached a letter presenting our findings and recommendations for the host
city of the 2029 Super Bowl to the NFL, including advantages for selecting Stanford CA
and Denver CO as the host cities.

Keywords: Super Bowl, Combine Weighting Method, Sustainability, AHP, VIKOR
